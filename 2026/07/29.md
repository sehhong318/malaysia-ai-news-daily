#### 2. Malaysia's nominal GDP reached RM2.025 trillion in 2025

- **Source:** [Department of Statistics Malaysia](https://www.dosm.gov.my/portal-main/release-content/gross-domestic-product-income-approach-2025)
- **Published:** 2026-07-29
- **Why it matters:** The income breakdown shows how Malaysia's economic growth was distributed between employees, business surpluses and net taxes after subsidy reductions.
