### Malaysia News

#### 1. Malaysia and WIPO launch data model for measuring the creative economy

- **Source:** [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.com.my/2026/07/25/myipo-and-wipo-launch-creative-economy-data-model-aimed-at-supporting-policy-formulation/)
- **Published:** 2026-07-25
- **Why it matters:** More systematic measurement could help Malaysia design evidence-based policy for a sector that already accounts for a material share of national output and employment.

The Intellectual Property Corporation of Malaysia and the World Intellectual Property Organization launched the Creative Economy Data Model to support policy formulation, development planning and measurement of the sector's economic impact. MyIPO said the model updates a collaboration that began with a 2008 copyright-industries study and is intended to capture digital transformation, new business models and technologies including AI. Citing the Department of Statistics Malaysia's 2024 Culture and Creative Satellite Account, the report puts the creative economy at 6.8% of GDP, or about RM130.7 billion, and more than 760,000 jobs.

#### 2. Anwar says Malaysia will announce decision on possible Formula 1 return

- **Source:** [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/07/26/govt-to-announce-f1-news-today)
- **Published:** 2026-07-26
- **Why it matters:** A Formula 1 return would revive a major international event at Sepang, but its public cost and commercial terms would require scrutiny.

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim said the government would make an announcement on Sunday about speculation that Malaysia could host a Formula 1 race. The Star reported that Malaysia and Türkiye had been discussed as possible October hosts amid disruption to the season's final races in Qatar and Abu Dhabi caused by the Middle East conflict. No official decision or financial terms had been announced as of this edition; Malaysia last hosted Formula 1 at Sepang in 2017 before the race was dropped over hosting and licensing costs.

### AI News

#### 1. Samsung and Broadcom plan more than US$200 billion AI-chip collaboration

- **Source:** [Samsung Global Newsroom](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-electronics-and-broadcom-expand-strategic-collaboration-across-memory-and-foundry-technologies)
- **Published:** 2026-07-25
- **Why it matters:** The proposed scale and breadth of the partnership could strengthen Samsung's position as an integrated alternative supplier of memory, advanced logic and packaging for AI infrastructure.

Samsung Electronics and Broadcom signed a memorandum of understanding to expand collaboration across memory and foundry technologies, estimating its value at more than US$200 billion over five years through 2030. The companies plan to work on high-bandwidth memory for Broadcom AI accelerators, Samsung processes at two nanometres and below, and 2.3D and 2.5D advanced packaging. The value and future scope are company estimates under an MOU rather than completed purchases or independently verified deployments.
