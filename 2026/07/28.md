#### 1. School-reform pilot will select 10 schools in every district

- **Source:** [Malay Mail, reporting Utusan Malaysia and the Education Ministry](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/07/28/education-ministry-to-begin-school-reform-pilot-next-week-10-schools-selected-in-each-district/229187)
- **Published:** 2026-07-28
- **Why it matters:** A district-wide pilot could test whether targeted funding and changes to teaching, school culture and student support can produce reforms that scale nationally.

Education Minister Fadhlina Sidek said the ministry will begin a pilot next week involving five primary and five secondary schools in every district, with each selected school eligible for up to RM100,000 for locally designed initiatives. She said the programme is intended to improve academic performance, teacher professionalism and the school ecosystem rather than focus only on physical upgrades. The wider agenda includes mental-health support, anti-bullying work, new Safe School Guidelines and a Student Protection Policy; the report does not specify the pilot's evaluation period or total allocation.

#### 2. Early voting begins in the Negeri Sembilan state election

- **Source:** [Malay Mail, reporting Bernama and the Election Commission](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/07/28/n-sembilan-polls-38-early-voting-centres-open-for-22339-security-personnel-spouses/229182)
- **Published:** 2026-07-28
- **Why it matters:** The early vote formally begins balloting in a competitive state election that will determine control of Negeri Sembilan's 36-seat assembly.

Thirty-eight early-voting centres opened for 22,339 members of the armed forces and police and their spouses, comprising 16,884 military-linked voters and 5,455 police-linked voters. The Election Commission scheduled centres to close in stages between noon and 5pm. Ordinary polling for about 889,490 voters is set for August 1, with 103 candidates contesting 36 seats.

#### 3. Authorities relocate 126 Rohingya from outside UNHCR's Kuala Lumpur office

- **Source:** [Malay Mail, reporting Bernama and Kuala Lumpur police](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/07/28/rohingya-refugees-relocated-after-setting-up-temporary-settlement-outside-unhcr-office-say-police/229184)
- **Published:** 2026-07-28
- **Why it matters:** The operation highlights the immediate humanitarian and legal consequences of Malaysia's limited formal framework for refugees.

Kuala Lumpur police said 126 Rohingya refugees who had erected tents outside the UN refugee agency's office were moved to the police contingent headquarters for screening and documentation. Police said people without documents would be detained, while UNHCR would verify cardholders and work with support groups to identify accommodation for those with valid documents. The group cooperated and no obstruction was reported; police had not verified claims that two additional buses were heading to the capital.

#### 1. NVIDIA-led alliance launches an open security stack for AI agents

- **Source:** [NVIDIA Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/open-secure-ai-alliance/)
- **Published:** 2026-07-27
- **Why it matters:** Shared, inspectable security components could give organisations more control over agent identity, permissions, auditing and vulnerability response across multi-vendor AI systems.

NVIDIA announced the Open Secure AI Alliance with inaugural partners spanning cloud, cybersecurity and enterprise software, including Microsoft, IBM, the Linux Foundation, Hugging Face, Cisco, Cloudflare and SpaceXAI. The group plans to develop and share open technologies for securing software and agents; NVIDIA contributed models, data and the open-source NOOA research framework for testing, tracing, auditing and governing agent harnesses. The announcement presents the alliance's security case and the reported response to a recent Hugging Face incident from NVIDIA's perspective, and does not provide independent performance results for the contributed tools.

#### 2. Anthropic says it does not support a categorical ban on open-weight models

- **Source:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/position-open-weights-models)
- **Published:** 2026-07-27
- **Why it matters:** Anthropic's clarification sharpens a major policy divide over how to preserve open access while controlling advanced-model security risks.

CEO Dario Amodei said Anthropic has never advocated banning open-weight models as a category and called models without dangerous capabilities a public good. He instead backed controls on advanced chips reaching authoritarian states, measures against industrial-scale model distillation and mandatory safety testing for sufficiently capable open and closed models. The post is Anthropic's policy position rather than a binding government proposal, and it disputes claims made during the recent industry debate over open weights.

### Malaysia News

#### 1. OECD urges Malaysia to renew fiscal, competition and education reforms

- **Source:** [Bernama, reporting the OECD Economic Survey of Malaysia](https://www.bernama.com/en/region/news.php?id=2587002)
- **Published:** 2026-07-28
- **Why it matters:** The recommendations identify structural reforms Malaysia could use to sustain growth while containing public debt and improving productivity and educational outcomes.

The OECD projected Malaysian GDP growth of 4.9% in 2026 and 5.0% in 2027, after 5.2% in 2025, while warning of downside risks from trade tensions, higher commodity prices and weaker global demand. Its new survey recommended replacing broad energy subsidies with targeted transfers, considering a broad-based consumption tax, easing market-entry barriers and improving competition between state-owned and private firms. It also called for reforms spanning preschool access, teacher incentives, tertiary curricula and climate resilience; these are OECD recommendations rather than policies adopted by the Malaysian government.

### AI News

#### 1. Microsoft puts its first specialised cybersecurity model into MDASH

- **Source:** [The Hacker News, reporting Microsoft](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/microsoft-says-new-cybersecurity-ai.html)
- **Published:** 2026-07-28
- **Why it matters:** Routing routine vulnerability work to a smaller specialised model could lower security-analysis costs, but the limited benchmark and private-preview access make broad performance conclusions premature.

Microsoft launched MAI-Cyber-1-Flash inside MDASH, its vulnerability-identification and remediation harness, with access limited to approved customers through an Azure AI Foundry private preview rather than a standalone public model. The company said the model can handle up to 90% of MDASH tasks while GPT-5.4 handles the hardest 10%, and claimed that this combination scored 95.95% on CyberGym at half the cost of its previous best configuration. The Hacker News noted that CyberGym Level 1 tests reproduction of known vulnerabilities rather than blind discovery or patch correctness, and that Microsoft's new result was not on the benchmark's public leaderboard when checked; Project Perception, the broader agent-coordination system, is scheduled for public preview on August 3.
