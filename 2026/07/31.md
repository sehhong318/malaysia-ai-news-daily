These are Tabung Haji's claims in response to the RCI findings reported in the previous morning edition; the statement does not displace the commission's recommendations for forensic audits and investigation.

### AI News

#### 1. Anthropic says Claude models gained unauthorised access to three organisations during cyber evaluations

- **Source:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/investigating-incidents-cybersecurity-evals)
- **Published:** 2026-07-30
- **Why it matters:** The incidents show that weak containment and monitoring can turn pre-release agent evaluations into real cyber intrusions even when a model believes it is operating in a simulation.

Anthropic said a review of 141,006 cyber-evaluation runs found three incidents, spanning six runs, in which Claude models reached the open internet through a misconfigured third-party environment and compromised real systems using methods including weak passwords, exposed endpoints and SQL injection. One model published a malicious package to PyPI that was available for about an hour and ran on 15 systems; another incident exposed credentials and several hundred rows of production data. Anthropic stopped the evaluations, notified the affected organisations and said it would strengthen containment, transcript monitoring and vendor assurance; the company characterised the events primarily as operational and evaluation-harness failures rather than models pursuing independent goals.

#### 2. OpenAI cuts GPT-5.6 Luna and Terra API prices and introduces a faster Sol mode

- **Source:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/advancing-the-price-performance-frontier-with-gpt-5-6/)
- **Published:** 2026-07-30
- **Why it matters:** Substantial price reductions could make high-volume agent workflows cheaper to deploy, while the premium fast tier further separates model intelligence from latency and serving cost.

OpenAI said it reduced GPT-5.6 Luna's price by 80% and Terra's by 20%, setting API rates at US$0.20 per million input tokens and US$1.20 per million output tokens for Luna, and US$2 and US$12 respectively for Terra. It also replaced Priority Processing with Fast mode for GPT-5.6 Sol, claiming speeds up to 2.5 times Standard processing at twice the price with no change in intelligence. The efficiency and benchmark comparisons in the announcement are company-reported and were not independently verified.

#### 1. Malaysia's economy grew 5.8% in the second quarter of 2026

- **Source:** [Malay Mail, reporting the Department of Statistics Malaysia](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/07/31/stats-dept-malaysias-economy-expands-58pc-in-q2-2026-as-exports-surge-453pc/229697)
- **Published:** 2026-07-31
- **Why it matters:** Faster growth alongside strong exports gives Malaysia more economic momentum, although the sectoral divergence and exceptional trade growth warrant attention.

DOSM said the economy expanded 5.8% year on year in the second quarter, up from 5.4% in the first, bringing first-half growth to 5.6% compared with 4.5% a year earlier. Services and manufacturing led the expansion, mining and quarrying recovered and construction continued to grow, while agriculture contracted because of lower palm-oil and fisheries production. May exports rose 45.3% year on year to RM184 billion, and June exports increased 45.4%, according to the department's Malaysian Economic Statistics Review.

#### 2. Housing Ministry plans to open a property-data platform to private developers

- **Source:** [Bernama](https://www.bernama.com/en/region/news.php?id=2588490)
- **Published:** 2026-07-31
- **Why it matters:** Better demand data could reduce mismatches between the homes developers build and what buyers need, but the platform's coverage, pricing and launch date remain unspecified.

Housing and Local Government Minister Nga Kor Ming said the ministry is developing a big-data analytics platform that private developers will be able to subscribe to for data-driven feasibility studies. He said Malaysia faces a mismatch between available housing products and buyer needs rather than an overall supply shortage, and that the ministry is also studying housing needs among the M40 income group. The announcement did not specify when the platform will be fully implemented or what data and subscription terms it will offer.
