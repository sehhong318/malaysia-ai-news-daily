#### 1. Formula 1 confirms Sepang will host the 2026 Bahrain Grand Prix, subject to final approvals

- **Source:** [Mercedes-AMG PETRONAS F1 Team](https://www.mercedesamgf1.com/news/sepang-international-circuit-to-host-2026-bahrain-grand-prix-in-malaysia)
- **Published:** 2026-07-26
- **Why it matters:** The confirmation brings Formula 1 back to Sepang after nine years, while the remaining approvals and financial arrangements still warrant scrutiny.

Formula 1 president and chief executive Stefano Domenicali confirmed that Malaysia will host the Bahrain Grand Prix at Sepang as round 16 of the 2026 championship, between the Azerbaijan and Singapore races. The event will retain Bahrain naming and branding, but the announcement says it remains subject to final agreements, official sign-off and World Motor Sport Council approval. This materially updates the previous evening's report, which said an agreement and October 2–4 dates had been reported but not officially confirmed.

#### 2. MACC to host regional anti-corruption meeting in Melaka

- **Source:** [Malay Mail, reporting Bernama and MACC](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/07/27/malaysia-to-strengthen-regional-anti-graft-cooperation-through-asean-pac-meeting/229071)
- **Published:** 2026-07-27
- **Why it matters:** The meeting gives Southeast Asian enforcement agencies a forum to coordinate on emerging corruption risks and integrity controls for major infrastructure projects.

The Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission will host the 22nd ASEAN Parties Against Corruption Secretariat Meeting in Melaka from July 28 to 30 during Malaysia's 2025–2026 chairmanship. MACC said delegates from all ASEAN members, including Timor-Leste, and representatives from bodies including the UN Office on Drugs and Crime, OECD and Asian Development Bank will discuss enforcement, prevention, capacity building and governance. A seminar will focus specifically on corruption-risk management and integrity in large infrastructure projects; no binding regional measures were announced in the cited report.

NVIDIA's performance and benchmark claims, including Nemotron 3 Ultra's reported results in agentic register-transfer-level coding, are company-reported rather than independently verified here.
