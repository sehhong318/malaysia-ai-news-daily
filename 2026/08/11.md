- **Published:** 2026-08-10
- **Why it matters:** Clearer rules for property development, building managers, tenancies and strata governance could materially affect owners, renters and nearly three million strata units.

Housing and Local Government Minister Nga Kor Ming said the National Housing Policy 2026–2035 includes proposed Property Development, Building Managers and Rent Tenancy Acts plus amendments to the Strata Management Act 2013. #### 1. Meta releases the 30-billion-parameter Muse Glimmer model under Apache 2.0

- **Source:** [Meta AI Research](https://research.meta.ai/blog/introducing-muse-glimmer-open-agentic-model)
- **Published:** 2026-08-10
- **Why it matters:** A permissively licensed multimodal agent model designed for local hardware broadens access to private, offline and customisable agentic workflows.

Meta released Muse Glimmer's weights under Apache 2.0, describing it as a 30-billion-parameter model for local tool use, coding, long-horizon tasks, failure recovery and text-image input. Benchmark and minimal-degradation claims are Meta's own and require independent replication.

- **Why it matters:** Giving selected defenders fewer-restriction cyber models may improve vulnerability research, but also makes access controls and monitoring more consequential.
