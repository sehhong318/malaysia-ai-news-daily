#### 1. National housing policy proposes life-cycle cost disclosure for strata homes

- **Source:** [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/08/26/national-housing-policy-clearer-long-term-maintenance-costs-for-strata-properties-stronger-management-rules/231691)
- **Published:** 2026-08-26
- **Why it matters:** Showing long-term upkeep and replacement costs before purchase could improve buyer decisions and pressure strata managers to budget sustainably.

Malaysia's National Housing Policy 2026–2035 proposes adding estimated maintenance and component-replacement costs over a strata property's life cycle to sale and purchase agreements. It also calls for performance assessment of strata management, more professional building managers and amendments to the Strata Management Act 2013. These are policy proposals whose final legal form and implementation timetable remain pending.

#### 2. Health Ministry assesses an HFMD vaccine despite a mismatch with prevalent strains

- **Source:** [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/08/26/vax-for-hfmd-being-assessed)
- **Published:** 2026-08-26
- **Why it matters:** A vaccine decision must balance a large childhood disease burden against whether a single-strain product fits the viruses currently circulating in Malaysia.

The Health Ministry is evaluating the role of an EV-A71 hand, foot and mouth disease vaccine, but said EV-A71 represented only 1.5% of enteroviruses detected so far this year. Malaysia recorded 40,879 HFMD cases through August 22, almost 83% fewer than the 239,907 cases in the same period last year. A local company is seeking regulatory review to import the China-made vaccine before possible local production, so approval and national use are not yet settled.

#### 1. OpenAI publishes first Jalapeño inference-system benchmarks

- **Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/25/openais-jalapeno-chip-is-built-for-fast-inference-at-scale-benchmarks-show/)
- **Published:** 2026-08-25
- **Why it matters:** A competitive in-house inference platform could reduce OpenAI's power costs and dependence on Nvidia, but only once it reaches meaningful deployment scale.

OpenAI said at Hot Chips that its Broadcom-developed Jalapeño system delivered more tokens per user and more throughput per kilowatt than currently available processors in SemiAnalysis's InferenceX benchmark, including an Nvidia Blackwell system. Results are company-reported, and only very small volumes are expected by end-2026 before a larger 2027 deployment.
