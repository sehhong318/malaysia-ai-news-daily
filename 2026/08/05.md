### Malaysia News

#### 1. Malaysia plans to expand protected farmland as data-centre and chip industries compete for land

- **Source:** [The Edge Malaysia, carrying Bloomberg reporting](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/813357)
- **Published:** 2026-08-05
- **Why it matters:** Protecting productive land could help Malaysia balance food security against the rapid land demand generated by data centres, semiconductor manufacturing and urban growth.

Deputy Agriculture and Food Security Minister Chan Foong Hin said Malaysia is working with state governments to protect farmland from industrial and property development and plans to propose expanding protected arable land, although he gave no target or Cabinet timetable. About 25,000 hectares—nearly 5% of Malaysian farmland excluding oil-palm and rubber plantations—has already been designated under the Permanent Food Production Zones programme, according to the report. Because state and local authorities administer land use, the proposal is not yet a completed nationwide policy change.

### AI News

#### 1. World Bank says AI could augment 16.2% of developing-economy jobs

- **Source:** [World Bank Group](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/08/04/ai-offers-lifeline-to-developing-economies-in-an-era-of-weak-growth)
- **Published:** 2026-08-04
- **Why it matters:** The report frames affordable, locally adapted AI as a potential development tool while warning that weak infrastructure and institutions could instead widen inequality between and within countries.

The World Bank's *World Development Report 2026* estimates that AI could meaningfully boost productivity in 16.2% of jobs in developing economies, close to 18.7% in high-income countries. It estimates generative-AI automation risk at 4.5% of existing jobs in low- and middle-income countries versus 14.2% in high-income countries. The report recommends that developing countries first adopt available tools, then adapt them locally and only later advance towards frontier development, while investing in power, connectivity, skills, local data and institutions.

#### 2. Cloudflare announces identity and stablecoin-wallet tools for AI agents

- **Source:** [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/press/press-releases/2026/cloudflare-gives-ai-agents-an-identity-and-a-wallet/)
- **Published:** 2026-08-04
- **Why it matters:** Identity, authorization and bounded payments are basic infrastructure challenges if autonomous agents are to transact online with accountability.

Cloudflare announced account-linked web identities, an Account Wallet that can hold stablecoins, and assignable Virtual Wallets intended to let AI agents make purchases within owner-set limits. The company says controls will include spending caps, approved-merchant lists and maximum transaction sizes. Handle reservations opened on August 4, but full wallet access—including funding, withdrawals and Virtual Wallet issuance—is only promised for the coming months, so the complete system is not yet generally available.
