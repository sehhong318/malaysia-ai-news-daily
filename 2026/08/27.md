- **Why it matters:** Timely sanctions-list updates are a core control against prohibited customers and transactions across both banking and digital-payment services.

#### 6. High Court says bank automation does not displace a duty of care

- **Source:** [The Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/815800)
- **Published:** 2026-08-26
- **Why it matters:** The ruling may influence how Malaysian banks design name-and-account matching safeguards for corporate electronic payments.

The High Court ordered Alliance Bank Malaysia and Alliance Islamic Bank to pay RM1.163 million plus interest to Yatt Fatt Auto Supplies after transfers went to a wrong account despite the intended recipient's correct name being entered. Judicial Commissioner Moh Kok Wai held that a system requiring two identifiers should exercise reasonable care by detecting obvious contradictions rather than operationally ignoring one. The decision concerns the facts of this case and may still be subject to appeal.
