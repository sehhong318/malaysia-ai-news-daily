#### 1. GLICs target about RM30 billion in domestic investment under GEAR-uP in 2026

- **Source:** [The Malaysian Reserve](https://themalaysianreserve.com/2026/08/07/gear-up-targets-rm30b-domestic-investments-in-2026/)
- **Published:** 2026-08-07
- **Why it matters:** The planned deployment could anchor private capital and expand Malaysian capacity in semiconductors, advanced manufacturing, mid-tier companies and the energy transition.

Government-linked investment companies are targeting about RM30 billion in domestic investment under GEAR-uP this year, after deploying RM20.3 billion in 2025, Finance Minister II Amir Hamzah Azizan said. The target contributes to a broader commitment of RM120 billion in additional domestic direct investment from 2024 through 2028, although Amir Hamzah said the government places more importance on crowding in capital and creating sustainable opportunities than hitting the exact figure. The report said RM3.2 billion of the 2025 deployment went to advanced technology and semiconductors, while RM1.8 billion went to mid-tier scale-ups.

#### 2. Malaysia and Singapore discuss stronger gig-worker protection and labour cooperation

- **Published:** 2026-08-07
- **Why it matters:** Cooperation between the closely linked labour markets could shape worker protections and skills policy as platform work and AI change employment arrangements.

Human Resources Minister R Ramanan said Malaysia and Singapore discussed improving gig-worker welfare and protection during his meeting with Singapore Acting Minister for Manpower Jasmin Lau. The talks also covered labour-market changes, talent development, upskilling and preparing workers for technological advances and AI. The report describes areas for continued cooperation and exchange of best practices, not a signed agreement or newly enacted worker-protection rule.

### AI News

#### 1. OpenAI pauses some Astra work while assessing possible “critical” cyber capability

- **Source:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/responding-next-frontier-critical-cyber-capabilities/); [Reuters via Euronext](https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/openai-flags-possible-critical-cybersecurity-risk-upcoming-model-tightens-controls)
- **Published:** 2026-08-07
- **Why it matters:** A frontier model potentially reaching OpenAI's highest disclosed cyber-risk threshold would raise the stakes for secure development, external evaluation and controlled deployment.

OpenAI said preliminary evaluations and external assessments were strong enough that it could not rule out its forthcoming Astra model having “critical” cybersecurity capability, which under its framework includes autonomous exploitation of severe real-world vulnerabilities or complex attacks on highly secured targets. Reuters reported that the company paused internal Astra activities that did not meet strengthened security requirements and moved development into isolated environments with restricted network access and sandboxed execution. The assessment remains preliminary: OpenAI has not said Astra definitively meets the threshold and said the model was not involved in the Hugging Face incident.

#### 2. Anthropic says revised biology safeguards cut Fable 5 fallbacks by about 85%

- **Source:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/improving-fable-5-s-biology-safeguards)
- **Published:** 2026-08-07
- **Why it matters:** The change illustrates the difficult trade-off between making advanced biology assistance useful and preventing dual-use capabilities from enabling serious harm.

Anthropic said a revised safety classifier reduced biology-related “fallbacks” to a less capable model by about 85% in its testing, enabling Fable 5 to answer more benign health, clinical and educational queries. Requests involving areas it classifies as dual-use—including virology, toxicology and molecular design—will still fall back to Opus 5, so Anthropic says Fable 5 is not yet usable for professional biology research and drug development. The 85% figure is the company's own test result, and the post does not provide independent real-world validation.

#### 1. Alibaba reportedly plans revenue sharing for large commercial users of Qwen3.8-Max

- **Source:** [Yahoo Finance/Quartz, citing Reuters](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/alibaba-plans-revenue-sharing-next-131417995.html)
- **Published:** 2026-08-07
- **Why it matters:** Charging large self-hosted users would test a new commercial model for open-weight frontier systems and could reshape deployment costs for AI businesses.

Alibaba plans to require large commercial users of the open-weight Qwen3.8-Max model to share part of the revenue generated from it, according to Reuters reporting cited by Quartz. The model's open weights were expected the following week, but the proposed revenue-share rate remained under negotiation; Alibaba had previously charged for cloud access while generally allowing self-hosted open-weight use without a fee. The plan is not yet final, and Alibaba did not comment in the cited report.
