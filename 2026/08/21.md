#### 6. Energy Commission sets detailed rules for residential net-metered solar

- **Source:** [Suruhanjaya Tenaga](https://www.st.gov.my/sites/default/files/2026-08/GUIDELINES-FOR-SOLAR-PHOTOVOLTAIC-INSTALLATION-UNDER-THE-PROGRAMME-OF-NEM-RAKYAT-AND-NEM-GOMEN-IN-PENINSULAR-MALAYSIA_Rev.1_2026_.pdf)
- **Published:** 2026-08-19
- **Why it matters:** Clear capacity, credit and connection rules give households and public facilities a firmer basis for rooftop-solar investment while limiting projects to network constraints.

The Energy Commission's guidelines generally require applicants to be Tenaga Nasional customers and place panels on the same premises: NEM Rakyat is capped at 5 kWac for single-phase and 12.5 kWac for three-phase homes, while NEM GoMEn permits up to 1,000 kW subject to technical limits. Surplus generation earns a one-for-one consumption credit that can carry forward for 12 months, and the net-metering arrangement lasts 10 years before switching to self-consumption. Installations above 72 kW require a NEM Assessment Study, while all systems remain subject to electrical, safety and network-connection requirements.

- **Source:** [Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/newlyprojected-energy-demand-created-by-ai-and-chips-calls-for-19-more-nuclear-reactors/12835871)
- **Published:** 2026-08-20
- **Why it matters:** AI and semiconductor plans are forcing national energy systems to reconcile industrial growth with generation, grid, emissions and project-execution constraints.

South Korea's projected 2040 peak electricity demand rose by 26.6–26.8 GW from an estimate issued four months earlier, roughly the capacity of 19 APR1400 nuclear reactors. The government attributed the revision mainly to semiconductor projects and AI data centres: incremental data-centre demand nearly tripled from 4 GW to 11.9 GW, while only the specified first phase of projects was included. These are planning forecasts with substantial uncertainty, not approved construction of 19 reactors.

#### 3. Pew estimates one in ten sampled web pages shows significant AI authorship

- **Source:** [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/data-labs/2026/08/20/how-much-of-the-internet-is-written-with-ai/)
- **Published:** 2026-08-20
- **Why it matters:** A rising synthetic share of the public web affects trust, search quality, provenance and the future data available to train models.

Pew analysed nearly 500,000 English-language pages collected from Common Crawl over five years with the Open Pangram detector. In its July 2026 sample, 10% showed significant signs of AI authorship; among pages published after ChatGPT's release, the share exceeded one-third, while rates varied substantially by domain type. Pew cautions that detectors can misclassify individual pages, so the estimates describe aggregate patterns rather than verified authorship of each document.

#### 4. McGill researchers reduce the cost of uncertainty-aware neural networks

- **Source:** [McGill University](https://www.mcgill.ca/newsroom/channels/news/mcgill-researchers-develop-more-efficient-way-identify-when-ai-responses-may-need-human-review-373879)
- **Published:** 2026-08-20
- **Why it matters:** Cheaper uncertainty estimates could help deployed systems flag unfamiliar inputs for human review instead of returning unsupported confidence.

McGill researchers introduced “Singular Bayesian Neural Networks,” an approach intended to preserve uncertainty estimates while reducing the computation and memory burden of Bayesian models. In one experiment it used about 33 times fewer parameters than a commonly used uncertainty-estimation method while retaining strong predictive performance; the peer-reviewed work was presented at ICML 2026. The result is experimental, and the team is still working to automate which network components should be retained for different tasks.

#### 6. Liquid AI releases speculative-decoding models with claimed speedups up to 3.18×

- **Source:** [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/20/liquid-ai-releases-lfm2-5-dspark-draft-models-that-deliver-up-to-3-18x-faster-decoding/)
- **Published:** 2026-08-20
- **Why it matters:** Faster exact-output decoding can lower latency for local agents and coding tools without changing the target model, although gains depend heavily on workload and hardware.

Liquid AI released DSpark draft checkpoints for three LFM2.5 models, using roughly 300-million-parameter drafters to propose token blocks that the target verifies in one pass. It reports up to 3.18× speedup on an H100 and 2.87× on an M4 Max under greedy decoding, with day-one llama.cpp and SGLang support; average gains varied widely, and the 8B-A1B model averaged only 1.18× on the M4 Max. The results are vendor measurements, and the free commercial licence applies only to entities below US$10 million in annual revenue.
