#### 6. Anthropic begins watermarking Claude output under EU transparency rules

- **Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/some-claude-users-are-mad-that-anthropics-new-watermarks-will-catch-them-cheating-at-their-jobs-classes/)

- **Why it matters:** Machine-readable provenance could improve accountability for synthetic content, but it also raises reliability, privacy and interoperability questions for legitimate AI-assisted work.

Anthropic has started inserting invisible machine-readable markers into Claude-generated editorial text to comply with the EU AI Act's transparency requirements, prompting objections from some users and support from others. The system is intended to identify AI-generated or AI-edited output rather than claim ownership of it. Watermarks are not conclusive proof of authorship and may be weakened by editing or reprocessing, so they should not be treated as a standalone detector of misconduct.
