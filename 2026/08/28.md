#### 1. Malaysia schedules first Myanmar repatriation phase for September 29

- **Source:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/28/malaysia-to-return-1500-rohingya-refugees-next-month-despite-safety-fears)
- **Published:** 2026-08-28
- **Why it matters:** Returning Rohingya and other Myanmar nationals raises immediate non-refoulement, consent and safety questions while Malaysia manages a large detained and registered refugee population.

The Home Ministry said the first phase would involve 1,476 Myanmar nationals identified by Myanmar and willing to return voluntarily, using two Myanmar naval warships and a hospital ship. Malaysia plans to channel 4,008 people, mainly Rohingya, into its Refugee Registration Document programme and eventually repatriate 5,000; Human Rights Watch says conditions for safe, dignified return do not currently exist. The government has said it will not proceed where returnees face persecution or threats to life, so the voluntariness and individual screening will be central to implementation.

#### 4. Malaysia's first technology-based biomass credits head to a BCX auction

- **Source:** [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/esg/2026/08/28/biomass-carbon-credits-to-be-auctioned-on-bcx-in-september)
- **Published:** 2026-08-28
- **Why it matters:** A domestic industrial carbon project broadens Malaysia's voluntary market beyond forest credits, but the test will be whether credible supply can attract sustained buyer demand.

Bursa Carbon Exchange plans a September 29 auction for credits from the Verra-registered Malaysia Grouped Biomass Waste Recovery and Utilisation Project in Penang. BCX acknowledged that voluntary-market liquidity remains low because demand is limited.

#### 1. Anthropic previews a common standard for AI agents to operate physical equipment

- **Source:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-hardware-standard-research-preview)
- **Published:** 2026-08-27
- **Why it matters:** A model-agnostic interface for laboratories and factories could move agents from software workflows into consequential physical operations, making permissioning and safety evaluation more urgent.

Anthropic opened the Model Hardware Standard research preview to selected laboratories and manufacturers after developing it with HHMI Janelia Research Campus. MHS uses standard drivers and protocols, including MCP, to let agents coordinate programmable equipment such as microscopes, liquid handlers and robotic arms; Anthropic says partners reduced some integrations from weeks or months to hours or minutes. It remains an early preview, and the company plans to develop safety evaluations and best practices before open-sourcing the specification.
