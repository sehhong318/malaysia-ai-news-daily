### Malaysia News

#### 1. Malaysia plans higher defence spending as cyber, electromagnetic and regional risks grow

- **Source:** [Bernama](https://www.bernama.com/en/general/news.php?id=2596531)
- **Published:** 2026-08-19
- **Why it matters:** A planned rise toward 1.5% of GDP by 2030 would reshape national spending priorities and capabilities across both conventional and emerging security domains.

Defence Minister Mohamed Khaled Nordin said higher spending is driven by cyber and electromagnetic threats, mass misinformation, more military activity and the risk that regional incidents escalate. He said Malaysia will modernise the armed forces and gradually raise defence expenditure to 1.5% of GDP by 2030 while maintaining dialogue and ASEAN centrality. The target is a policy plan, and no new appropriation or detailed procurement list was announced.

#### 2. Malaysia voices concern over US-backed Philippine base close to Sabah

- **Source:** [The Straits Times/Bloomberg](https://www.straitstimes.com/asia/se-asia/malaysia-raises-concerns-over-us-backed-philippine-military-base)
- **Published:** 2026-08-19
- **Why it matters:** Military expansion near Sabah intersects with the unresolved Philippine claim and wider US–China competition, increasing the importance of deterrence and crisis communication.

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim cited concern over facilities being upgraded on Balabac island, one of nine Philippine sites accessible to US forces under the Enhanced Defense Cooperation Agreement and close to Sabah's Banggi island. He said Malaysia cannot match the scale of those facilities but needs stronger basic defensive capabilities, potentially requiring hundreds of millions or billions of ringgit. Malaysia and the Philippines continue to maintain close relations, and the report does not describe the site as directed at Malaysia.

#### 3. AWS survey finds Malaysian businesses want public-sector leadership on AI

- **Source:** [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2026/08/20/businesses-want-govt-leadership-in-ai-adoption)
- **Published:** 2026-08-20
- **Why it matters:** Practical government deployments, consistent rules and governance templates could determine whether business adoption moves from experimentation to durable productivity gains.

An Amazon Web Services survey of 1,000 members of the public and 1,000 business leaders found 72% of business respondents viewed government leadership as important to faster AI adoption. It reported business adoption rising from 27% to 38% over the past year, while respondents prioritised clearer regulatory guidance, implementation frameworks and workforce training. The adoption figures are AWS-sponsored survey results rather than official business-census measurements.

#### 4. Sandakan fire destroys 28 stilt houses while crews save 162

Sabah's Fire and Rescue Department said 28 non-permanent stilt houses were destroyed in an early-morning blaze at Kampung Nunuyan Laut on Nunuyan Laut Island. Firefighters reported saving 162 other houses after receiving the emergency call at 3:50am. The published account did not establish the cause of the fire.

#### 5. Infomina agrees to buy 51% of cybersecurity firm Blue Fortress for RM25.5 million

- **Source:** [The Star, citing a Bursa Malaysia filing](https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2026/08/19/infomina-to-acquire-51-stake-in-blue-fortress-for-rm255mil)
- **Published:** 2026-08-19
- **Why it matters:** The purchase adds cybersecurity and digital-trust capabilities to a Malaysian enterprise-technology group serving banks, large companies and government agencies.

Infomina said its wholly owned subsidiary will acquire a 51% stake in Blue Fortress for RM25.5 million. Blue Fortress provides software, consultancy, project management and information-security services, and Infomina said the deal complements its infrastructure, digital-transformation, data and AI businesses. The company expects a positive future contribution, but that remains a management forecast rather than a realised result.

#### 6. Gas Malaysia's second-quarter profit falls as prices and costs offset higher volumes

- **Source:** [The Star, citing a Bursa Malaysia filing](https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2026/08/19/gas-malaysia-reports-lower-2q26-earnings)
- **Published:** 2026-08-19
- **Why it matters:** Weaker margins at a major gas distributor show that higher sales volume does not fully protect earnings from lower prices, operating expenses and financing costs.

Gas Malaysia reported second-quarter net profit of RM72.53 million, down from RM99.12 million a year earlier, while revenue declined to RM1.58 billion from RM1.8 billion. It attributed the revenue drop mainly to a lower average natural-gas selling price, partly offset by higher volume; lower contribution margins, higher operating expenses and finance costs also reduced profit. The company declared a five-sen-per-share interim dividend payable on November 6.

- **Source:** [CNA/Reuters](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/business/marvell-gives-google-option-buy-122-billion-stake-in-custom-ai-chip-deal-6328781)
- **Published:** 2026-08-19
- **Why it matters:** The agreement expands Google's custom-chip supply chain beyond Broadcom and further intertwines cloud buyers with the semiconductor companies building their AI infrastructure.

#### 3. OpenAI previews cross-session safety monitoring that retains no customer data

- **Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/19/openai-seeks-to-one-up-anthropic-with-new-customer-privacy-protections/)
- **Published:** 2026-08-19
- **Why it matters:** Enterprise AI providers are trying to reconcile detection of multi-session abuse with customers' demands that sensitive prompts and outputs not be retained or manually reviewed.

OpenAI is previewing Private Safety Processing for selected customers, an automated system designed to detect abuse patterns across multiple sessions without retaining customer conversations. If triggered, it sends a narrowly defined signal to OpenAI, which may contact the customer for context; data sharing remains at the customer's discretion. The service is a limited preview, and its privacy and detection claims have not yet been independently audited in the available report.

#### 4. Technical error removes some researchers from OpenAI's vetted cyber programme

- **Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/19/researchers-complain-that-openai-revoked-their-access-to-limited-cyber-program/)
- **Published:** 2026-08-19
- **Why it matters:** Reliable, transparent access controls are essential when vetted defenders depend on less-restricted models for vulnerability research but the same capabilities could aid attackers.

At least five researchers told TechCrunch they lost access to OpenAI's Trusted Access for Cyber programme, including its Daybreak Blue tier. OpenAI attributed the revocations to a technical issue affecting a limited set of users and asked them to re-verify; all researchers interviewed were outside the US and Europe, though the geographic scope remains uncertain. #### 5. Cerebras unveils a three-wafer CS-4 rack delivering a claimed 750 PFLOPS

- **Source:** [Cerebras via GlobeNewswire/Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/cerebras-unveils-cs-4-30-000000723.html)
- **Published:** 2026-08-18
- **Why it matters:** Wafer-scale systems offer a specialised alternative to GPU clusters for low-latency inference, but cost, software support and independently measured workload performance will determine adoption.

Cerebras introduced the CS-4, a rack-scale system built from three WSE-3 Turbo processors with a stated 750 PFLOPS of AI compute, 129.6 petabytes per second of memory bandwidth and 7.2 terabits per second of I/O. The company claims up to twice the CS-3's speed, ten times its throughput per watt and 30 times the per-user token rate of GPU systems on one test, with first shipments planned this quarter. Those comparisons are vendor-reported and vary by model, context, precision and serving configuration.

#### 6. Mayo Clinic validates AI screening for heart obstruction from routine ultrasound video

- **Source:** [Mayo Clinic release via Mirage News](https://www.miragenews.com/mayo-clinic-ai-detects-heart-obstruction-via-1730034/)
- **Published:** 2026-08-19
- **Why it matters:** Screening from ordinary B-mode video could flag patients for confirmatory testing where specialised Doppler expertise is limited, but it is not a replacement for diagnosis.

Researchers trained a model to identify left-ventricular outflow-tract obstruction in hypertrophic cardiomyopathy from resting, non-Doppler ultrasound videos. The study included 1,833 Mayo Clinic patients, a 275-patient test group and external validation in 46 South Korean patients; results were published in *Circulation: Cardiovascular Imaging*. The team says prospective validation across broader populations and ultrasound platforms is still needed before routine use.

#### 7. TrueFoundry releases an MIT-licensed, model-neutral agent harness

- **Source:** [TrueForge on GitHub](https://github.com/truefoundry/trueforge)
- **Published:** 2026-08-19
- **Why it matters:** An open runtime for tools, sandboxes, approvals and session state can reduce dependence on a single model provider while making agent deployments more inspectable.

TrueFoundry released TrueForge, an open-source agent runtime covering model calls, MCP tools, skills, sandboxing, approvals, context management and session state, exposed through a chat interface, HTTP API and SDKs. It supports local SQLite operation and hosted deployments using Postgres, Redis, Docker Compose or Kubernetes; the project warns that local mode has no login and should remain on localhost. Its cost comparisons with managed agent services are maintainer-run benchmarks and require independent reproduction.
