#### 1. Highway charging bays fail to keep pace with Malaysia's EV fleet

- **Source:** [New Straits Times](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/news/nation/2026/08/1518254/malaysias-highway-charger-growth-lags-behind-ev-surge)
- **Published:** 2026-08-25
- **Why it matters:** Charging capacity on intercity routes and at dense housing could become a binding constraint on wider EV adoption despite falling vehicle prices.

The EV Owners Club said highway charging bays increased from 104 at end-2024 to 126 by mid-2026, while Malaysia's EV fleet rose from 39,000 to 122,286 over the same period, contributing to holiday queues. It attributed slow deployment to high costs, grid upgrades, fragmented approvals and weak commercial returns in rural areas, and proposed an empowered cross-ministry programme office. The figures and constraints are the club's assessment rather than an independently audited national infrastructure review.

#### 2. Seventeen areas placed under Level 1 hot-weather status

- **Source:** [The Star/Bernama](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/08/25/hot-weather-17-areas-under-level-1-status-says-metmalaysia)
- **Published:** 2026-08-25
- **Why it matters:** Sustained temperatures of at least 35°C across all three regions raise immediate health, water-demand and outdoor-work risks while haze remains active.

MetMalaysia placed 11 areas in Peninsular Malaysia, five in Sabah and Marudi in Sarawak under Level 1 caution status. The category applies when maximum daily temperatures reach 35°C to 37°C for at least three consecutive days. The status is updated daily and does not mean every location is experiencing a formally declared heatwave.

#### 3. Malaysia records 21.1 million first-half visitor arrivals, but growth slows

- **Source:** [New Straits Times/Bernama](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/news/nation/2026/08/1518065/malaysia-records-21mil-international-visitor-arrivals-1st-half-2026)
- **Published:** 2026-08-24
- **Why it matters:** Tourism is still expanding before Visit Malaysia 2026–2027, but weaker air connectivity shows how geopolitical shocks can quickly limit services-export growth.

Tourism Ministry and Immigration data showed 21,118,039 international arrivals in January–June, up 2.5% or 514,958 year on year, the slowest comparable growth since the post-pandemic recovery began. Middle East tensions were linked to 4,111 cancelled scheduled flights; 26 of 50 key source markets declined, although Singapore, the Philippines, China, Central Asia, Oceania and North America supported overall growth. The ministry has extended the Visit Malaysia campaign through 2027 and is shifting marketing towards Asian markets.

#### 4. Malaysia's Leading Index rises 1.3% but remains below its long-term trend threshold

- **Source:** [The Star/Bernama](https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2026/08/24/malaysia039s-leading-index-rises-13-in-june)
- **Published:** 2026-08-24
- **Why it matters:** Strong trade inputs point to continued near-term growth, while the sub-100 smoothed trend and weak company formation temper the signal.

The Department of Statistics Malaysia said the Leading Index reached 114.8 points in June, up 1.3% year on year and 0.02% month on month, driven chiefly by imports of precious and non-ferrous metals and semiconductors. The Coincident Index rose 2.6% year on year to 131.6, supported by Employees Provident Fund contributions and retail trade. DOSM nevertheless said the Leading Index's long-term smoothed trend remained below 100, so the headline rise is not an unqualified acceleration signal.

### AI News

#### 1. Meta opens a loss-tolerant RDMA transport for million-GPU Ethernet networks

- **Source:** [Engineering at Meta](https://engineering.fb.com/2026/08/24/networking-traffic/metaroce-rdma-transport-ai-ethernet/)
- **Published:** 2026-08-24
- **Why it matters:** An open transport layer that works on commodity Ethernet could reduce reliance on specialised fabrics as AI clusters scale across data centres.

Meta released the MetaRoCE specification, reference software and compliance suite through the Open Compute Project, moving path telemetry, packet ordering and congestion intelligence into network endpoints. In a 64-node AMD GPU test, Meta reported higher throughput and lower completion times than RoCEv2, about 86% throughput at 1% packet loss and linear scaling across four- and eight-plane topologies. These are developer-run results on the tested cluster, not independent evidence of million-GPU production performance.

#### 2. Microsoft links agent safety tests to portable runtime controls

- **Source:** [Microsoft Command Line](https://commandline.microsoft.com/safety-requirements-failure-paths-assert-acs/)
- **Published:** 2026-08-24
- **Why it matters:** Turning policy into repeatable evaluations and enforceable runtime rules addresses a practical gap between written AI governance and production behaviour.

Microsoft described an open-source workflow combining ASSERT, which generates and traces single- and multi-turn agent tests through OpenTelemetry, with Agent Control Specification controls such as Rego rules and model classifiers. In a synthetic banking demonstration, a generalised authorisation rule reduced violations to zero on the published 72-prompt test set, while separate semantic controls addressed coercive requests. The demonstration illustrates the tooling and is neither a production prevalence estimate nor a broad agent-safety benchmark.

#### 3. NVIDIA claims up to 30-fold agentic throughput per megawatt for Vera Rubin

- **Source:** [NVIDIA Technical Blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/vera-rubin-nvl72-efficiency-ai-agents/)
- **Published:** 2026-08-24
- **Why it matters:** If independently confirmed, sharply better inference per megawatt could ease the power and operating-cost bottleneck for long-running AI agents.

NVIDIA said on-silicon Vera Rubin NVL72 tests using recorded SemiAnalysis AgentX coding trajectories delivered up to 30 times the throughput per megawatt and up to 35 times lower token cost than GB300 NVL72 on DeepSeek V4 Pro. The company attributes the gains to rack-scale co-design, disaggregated serving, cache-aware routing, expert parallelism and power management. Results are NVIDIA-run, pending SemiAnalysis review and do not yet include Vera CPU tool-calling performance.

#### 4. AWS adds managed Ray training and serving to SageMaker HyperPod

- **Source:** [Amazon Web Services](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/introducing-new-ray-capabilities-on-sagemaker-hyperpod/)
- **Published:** 2026-08-24
- **Why it matters:** Managed fault recovery, observability and notebook access lower the operational burden of running distributed open-source AI workloads on large GPU clusters.

AWS integrated Ray and open-source KubeRay with SageMaker HyperPod on Amazon EKS, allowing developers to create clusters, submit jobs, connect JupyterLab or Code Editor and view Ray and Grafana dashboards from SageMaker Studio. The service adds node-health recovery, tiered checkpointing and storage-backed KV-cache offloading while retaining standard Ray APIs. Availability does not establish lower cost or better reliability for every workload, which will depend on cluster design and configuration.

#### 5. SLAC's AI compressor preserves selectable scientific detail at 10–100× smaller sizes

- **Source:** [SLAC release carried by Newswise](https://www.newswise.com/doescience/slac-researchers-develop-ai-based-tool-to-compress-data-without-losing-detail)
- **Published:** 2026-08-24
- **Why it matters:** Controllable compression could make data-intensive instruments usable when storage and transfer capacity cannot keep pace with experimental output.

SLAC researchers reported a neural compression method that uses wavelet analysis to separate features by scale before encoding them, allowing users to preserve fine scientific structure and decompress only a selected region. Across molecular, materials, solar-field and photographic data, the team said file sizes typically fell by 10 to 100 times depending on fidelity requirements; the work was published in *Nature Machine Intelligence*. The method supplements rather than replaces conventional selection and compression, and performance will vary by dataset and desired quality.
