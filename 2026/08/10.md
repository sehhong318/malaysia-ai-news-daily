- **Source:** [6abc/WPVI-TV](https://6abc.com/post/university-pennsylvania-researchers-develop-artificial-intelligence-tool-help-speed-autism-evaluations/19642585/)
- **Published:** 2026-08-09
- **Why it matters:** A quicker objective screening component could reduce assessment delays, but clinical use requires broader validation and must not be confused with a standalone diagnosis.

University of Pennsylvania researchers developed CAMI, a video-based system that scores how closely a child imitates movements shown on screen, a behavioural marker associated with autism. The team presents CAMI as one automated component of a full clinical evaluation, not a replacement for physicians or existing diagnostic processes.

- **Source:** [The Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/813782)
- **Published:** 2026-08-10
- **Why it matters:** Clearer rules on training data, ownership and attribution will shape whether Malaysia can support AI development without weakening the livelihoods of creators.

An analysis by Asia Tech Alliance and Wong & Partners argues that Malaysia can adapt its existing copyright, data-protection, consumer and platform laws rather than replace them with one rigid AI statute.
