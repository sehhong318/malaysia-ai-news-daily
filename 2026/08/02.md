#### 1. EU begins enforcing broader AI Act oversight and transparency duties

- **Source:** [European Commission](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1714); [JURIST](https://www.jurist.org/news/2026/08/european-commission-announces-ai-companies-to-face-tighter-eu-oversight-over-deepfakes-and-cyber-threats/)
- **Published:** 2026-07-31 (European Commission); 2026-08-02 (JURIST)
- **Why it matters:** The start of enforcement turns major transparency and general-purpose-model obligations into active regulatory requirements for companies operating in the EU.

From August 2, the European Commission's AI Office and national authorities begin enforcing the EU AI Act, while new transparency rules also start to apply. The rules include duties to make certain AI-generated or manipulated content machine-detectable and to disclose realistic synthetic images, audio or video; regulators can inspect models and technical records and require corrective action. The law does not prohibit all deepfakes, and its remaining provisions continue to phase in on separate timelines.
