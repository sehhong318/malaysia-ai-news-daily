- **Source:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/multiagent-systems)

- **Why it matters:** Systems made from individually capable agents can create new coordination failures and security risks that single-model evaluations do not capture.

Anthropic tested agent swarms on vulnerability discovery, collaborative game development and shared-code tasks. - **Why it matters:** OS-level least privilege and rollback can reduce the damage from prompt injection or autonomous mistakes more reliably than asking an agent to police its own behaviour.
