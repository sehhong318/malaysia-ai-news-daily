#### 2. California bill would bar AI chatbots from presenting themselves as therapists

- **Source:** [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/375725/california-bill-ban-ai-chatbots-therapy-mental-health)
- **Published:** 2026-08-16
- **Why it matters:** The proposal could become an important state-level boundary between administrative AI support and unlicensed mental-health treatment.

California Senate Bill 903 cleared the Assembly Appropriations Committee 13–0 and awaits an Assembly floor vote. It would limit AI in psychotherapy to support tasks, prohibit chatbots from posing as therapists and require clinician oversight and patient consent, while providing exemptions for areas including peer support, research and federally approved medical tools. The bill has not become law and, if amended by the Assembly, would still need Senate concurrence and the governor's signature.
