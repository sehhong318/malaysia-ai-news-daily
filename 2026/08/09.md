#### 2. New reasoning models still reproduce racial and gender stereotypes in medical scenarios

- **Source:** [Flinders University](https://news.flinders.edu.au/blog/2026/08/09/new-ai-models-still-reproduce-racial-and-gender-stereotypes-in-medicine/)
- **Published:** 2026-08-09
- **Why it matters:** Better reasoning-benchmark performance does not necessarily make clinical AI fairer, so deployment requires direct bias testing and monitoring.

Flinders University researchers generated 36,000 clinical vignettes with o3-mini and DeepSeek-R1 and found significant racial misrepresentation in 78% and 89% of tested conditions respectively, compared with 67% for GPT-4; gender misrepresentation was 56% and 67%, compared with 67% for GPT-4. The models overrepresented some groups in stereotypically associated conditions, leading the researchers to conclude that newer reasoning capabilities had not improved representational fairness. The results concern generated fictional vignettes, not measured diagnostic outcomes in deployed clinical systems.
