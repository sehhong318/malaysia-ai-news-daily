### Malaysia News

#### 1. Government rules out acquiring MyKad and passport supplier Datasonic

- **Source:** [The Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/815519)
- **Published:** 2026-08-22
- **Why it matters:** The clarification removes a major state-buyout scenario around critical identity infrastructure while keeping security, service quality and value-for-money under scrutiny.

The National Security Council said the government will not acquire Datasonic Technologies and will instead ensure identity services remain secure, reliable and cost-effective. This materially reverses the uncertainty reported on August 21, when NexG disclosed a government request for an indicative acquisition price and internally valued Datasonic at about RM7.5 billion. Datasonic remains the MyKad supplier and has supplied ICAO-compliant Malaysian passports since 2012.

#### 2. Malaysia targets the next parliamentary session for a new e-commerce Bill

- **Source:** [The Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/815522)
- **Published:** 2026-08-22
- **Why it matters:** Explicit duties for platforms and sellers could close accountability gaps as online commerce, scams and other digital harms expand.

Domestic Trade and Cost of Living Minister Armizan Mohd Ali said the planned law would replace the Electronic Commerce Act 2006, which validates electronic transactions but does not comprehensively assign legal responsibilities. His ministry is drafting provisions with the Attorney-General's Chambers, Communications Ministry, Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission and Digital Ministry, with tabling scheduled for the upcoming parliamentary session. The text and final timetable remain subject to the drafting and legislative process.

#### 3. Targeted-diesel subsidy improvements may be announced within a month

- **Source:** [The Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/815521)
- **Published:** 2026-08-22
- **Why it matters:** Easier access and more flexible eligibility could reduce unintended burdens from subsidy targeting, especially for households and users in Sabah and Sarawak.

Armizan said stakeholder feedback has reached the Finance Ministry and the government now expects to announce diesel-subsidy improvements in less than a month, earlier than the previously stated one-to-two-month window. Options under study include simplifying applications, relaxing eligibility involving vehicles acquired through informal hire-purchase takeovers and permitting quota transfers to immediate family members. These remain proposals until the government publishes the revised mechanism.

#### 7. Safe Internet Campaign shifts under-16 enforcement attention to parents

- **Source:** [Scoop, citing Bernama](https://www.scoop.my/news/296666/under-16-social-media-ban-needs-parents-not-just-platforms-to-succeed-teo/)
- **Published:** 2026-08-22
- **Why it matters:** Platform age checks alone cannot make the under-16 rule effective if adults create accounts or provide unsupervised devices at home.

Deputy Communications Minister Teo Nie Ching said the Safe Internet Campaign will prioritise parental supervision and guidance alongside enforcement of Malaysia's ban on social-media accounts for children aged 16 and below. #### 8. Perlis expedition records Malaysia's only known population of a black tokay gecko

- **Source:** [Malay Mail/Bernama](https://www.malaymail.com/news/life/2026/08/23/rare-black-tokay-gecko-found-only-in-one-state-in-malaysia-discovered-during-expedition/232294)
- **Published:** 2026-08-23
- **Why it matters:** The finding strengthens the scientific case for conserving Perlis's limestone and forest habitats while documenting the limits of the species' Malaysian range.

A biodiversity expedition recorded the black tokay gecko, *Gekko cf. dam*, in the Bukit Papan and Kurong Batang (South) forest-reserve area; researchers said it has so far been found only in Perlis within Malaysia, though it also occurs in Thailand. The team also found an orchid that may represent a new species and is still undergoing expert verification. The preliminary range and species assessments should not be treated as final taxonomic conclusions.

#### 3. RoboDojo exposes a large human–robot performance gap

- **Source:** [University of Hong Kong](https://www.hku.hk/press/press-releases/detail/29297.html); [AI Insider report](https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/08/22/hku-researchers-develop-unified-benchmark-for-physical-ai/)
- **Published:** 2026-08-22 (AI Insider report; HKU release dated 2026-08-12)
- **Why it matters:** Standardised simulation and real-robot testing can replace selective demonstrations with comparable evidence of whether embodied AI works reliably.

The HKU-led open benchmark covers 30 robot policies, 42 simulation tasks and 18 physical-robot tasks spanning generalisation, memory, precision and long-horizon execution. HKU reported that the best tested model succeeded on 8.8% of simulation tasks and 12.8% of real-world tasks, versus 76.03% and 100% for human experts. These are benchmark-specific results from the project team, and the underlying official release predates this edition even though fresh reporting surfaced on August 22.

#### 5. Study estimates AI-assisted writing in almost nine of ten December biomedical papers

- **Source:** [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02551-z)
- **Published:** 2026-08-20
- **Why it matters:** If the estimate holds, disclosure, peer review and research-integrity systems must adapt to AI use becoming normal rather than exceptional.

A preprint analysed English-language papers in PubMed Central and estimated signs of AI-assisted writing in almost 90% of papers published in December 2025, with annual estimates of 77% for 2025 and 52% for 2024. *Nature* notes that the study has not been peer reviewed and that its estimates are much higher than earlier work, making methodology and false-positive risks important. The figures indicate detected writing patterns, not proof that AI generated the science or conclusions in each paper.
