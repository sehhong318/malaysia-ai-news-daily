- **Published:** 2026-08-20
- **Why it matters:** If the estimate holds, disclosure, peer review and research-integrity systems must adapt to AI use becoming normal rather than exceptional.

The figures indicate detected writing patterns, not proof that AI generated the science or conclusions in each paper.
