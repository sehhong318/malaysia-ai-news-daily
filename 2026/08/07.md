#### 2. MIROS begins trial of advisory minimum speeds on three expressway stretches

- **Source:** [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/08/07/end-of-lane-hoggers-malaysia-trials-minimum-speed-limits-to-combat-highway-congestion/230297)
- **Published:** 2026-08-07
- **Why it matters:** The trial could inform a wider policy response to lane hogging, traffic bottlenecks and collision risk on Malaysian expressways.

The Malaysian Institute of Road Safety Research launched a proof-of-concept trial of advisory minimum speeds on stretches of the North-South Expressway and South Klang Valley Expressway, in collaboration with the Malaysian Highway Authority, PLUS and SKVE. Guidance ranges from at least 60 km/h in the left lane to 100 km/h in the rightmost lane on three- or four-lane highways, but MIROS stressed that the speeds are not legally enforceable and do not override weather, congestion or safety conditions. Data will be collected until October 31 before any decision on wider deployment.

### AI News

#### 1. Researchers use generative AI to design complete, functional bacteriophage genomes

- **Source:** [BBC News](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c5y3j3ngevmo)
- **Published:** 2026-08-06
- **Why it matters:** Designing whole functional genomes could advance phage therapies and synthetic biology, while creating urgent biosafety and biosecurity governance challenges.

Stanford researchers used the Evo1 and Evo2 genome models to generate bacteriophage designs, synthesised 302 selected candidates and found that 16 could replicate and kill *E. coli* in laboratory tests, BBC News reported. The work was limited to viruses that infect bacteria, with viruses capable of infecting complex organisms excluded from the training data, and was conducted in a secure laboratory. Researchers and independent experts said the milestone may support treatments for antibiotic-resistant infections but warned that extending generative genome design raises serious dual-use risks.
