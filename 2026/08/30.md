#### 5. Essay warns that AI benchmarks can become targets rather than reliable oversight

- **Source:** [Issues in Science and Technology](https://issues.org/ai-measurement-oversight-gasser-mayer-schonberger-marco/)
- **Published:** 2026-08-29
- **Why it matters:** If models and developers optimise to pass known tests, benchmark gains may overstate real-world safety and make adaptive, post-deployment inquiry more important.

Urs Gasser, Viktor Mayer-Schönberger and Fabienne Marco argue that AI oversight is vulnerable to a “measurement trap” because benchmarks operate inside the same competitive ecosystem as model development. This is a conceptual policy argument, not an empirical estimate of how often benchmark gaming occurs.
