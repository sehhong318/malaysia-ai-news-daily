#### 2. Study validates a five-part scale for trust in medical AI chatbots

- **Source:** [Scientific Reports](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-68299-8)
- **Published:** 2026-08-29
- **Why it matters:** A validated measurement tool can make patient trust more testable, but self-reported use is not evidence that medical chatbot advice is safe or clinically effective.

Researchers developed a 15-item scale spanning cognitive, affective, functional, human-like and interactional trust in AI medical conversational agents; the five factors explained 76.07% of variance in exploratory analysis. Interactional trust had the strongest association with higher self-reported use frequency, with an odds ratio of 3.096. The study validates a behavioural-research instrument, not diagnostic performance or patient outcomes.
