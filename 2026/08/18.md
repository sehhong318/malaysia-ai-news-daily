- **Source:** [NVIDIA Technical Blog](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/developing-nemotron-3-5-lightning-nvfp4-with-qad-using-nvidia-model-optimizer/)

- **Why it matters:** If independently reproduced, aggressive four-bit quantisation with limited quality loss could make capable agent models cheaper and easier to deploy on constrained hardware.

NVIDIA released an NVFP4 checkpoint of Nemotron 3.5 Lightning that it says reduces the model from 66 GB at full precision to 22 GB and delivers up to four times the throughput. The throughput and quality results are developer measurements and may vary by hardware, workload and serving configuration.
