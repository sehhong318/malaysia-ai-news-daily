### Malaysia News

#### 1. Government begins review of the RM1,700 minimum wage

- **Source:** [The Vibes](https://www.thevibes.com/articles/news/126291/govt-reviews-rm1700-minimum-wage-with-possible-increase-under-new-wage-formula)
- **Published:** 2026-08-18
- **Why it matters:** A higher statutory floor could lift low-paid workers' incomes, but the eventual rate must balance living costs, productivity and employers' ability to pay.

Human Resources Minister R. Ramanan said the government is reviewing whether to retain or raise the RM1,700 monthly minimum wage under the National Wages Consultative Council Act. The process will consider poverty-line income, median wages, consumer prices, productivity and unemployment, with the National Wages and Gig Economy Consultative Council to determine a proposed rate. No new minimum wage has yet been approved.

#### 2. July inflation eases to 1.8% as transport-price growth slows

- **Source:** [The Star, citing the Department of Statistics Malaysia](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/08/18/transport-price-drop-eases-july-inflation)
- **Published:** 2026-08-18
- **Why it matters:** Slower headline inflation offers some household relief, although faster food-price growth and still-elevated public-transport inflation show that pressures are uneven.

Malaysia's consumer-price inflation slowed to 1.8% in July from 1.9% in June, as transport inflation moderated to 1.4% from 2.8%, according to the Department of Statistics Malaysia. Food and beverage inflation, however, increased to 1.8% from 1.4%, while public-transport services remained 6.5% more expensive. The department said implementation of the Budi Diesel mechanism contributed to the slower transport increase.

#### 3. Cabinet committee targets a 50% reduction in road deaths by 2030

- **Source:** [The Star/Bernama](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/08/17/govt-targets-50-reduction-in-road-accident-deaths-by-2030)
- **Published:** 2026-08-17
- **Why it matters:** Malaysia lost 6,537 people on its roads last year, making measurable reductions—especially among motorcyclists—a major public-health and economic priority.

Deputy Prime Minister Ahmad Zahid Hamidi said the Cabinet Committee on Road Safety and Traffic Congestion set a target to halve road fatalities by 2030 and will strengthen cross-agency accountability. Motorcyclists and pillion riders represented about 66% of last year's deaths, while the estimated socioeconomic loss reached RM30 billion. Planned measures include the staged Safero heavy-vehicle safety programme and a data-led expansion of Socso's Op Cegah, but the source did not publish annual milestones for reaching the target.

#### 5. Norway's sovereign wealth fund cuts its Malaysia exposure by about one-third

- **Source:** [The Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/814832)
- **Published:** 2026-08-18
- **Why it matters:** The world's largest sovereign wealth fund sharply reduced Malaysian fixed-income holdings while increasing the technology share of its remaining local equity portfolio.

The Government Pension Fund Global's Malaysian holdings fell to US$2.89 billion at June 30 from US$4.27 billion at end-2025, according to The Edge's analysis of Norges Bank Investment Management disclosures. Malaysian government-bond holdings dropped to US$226.02 million from US$1.44 billion, while the fund held 174 listed companies worth US$2.66 billion, down from 200 worth US$2.80 billion. Its Malaysian technology-stock weighting nevertheless rose to about 15% from 8.5%; the disclosures show portfolio changes but do not establish the fund's motives for each trade.

#### 4. Autonomous security agent exploits a Snowflake workflow flaw missed by AI review

- **Source:** [Wiz Research](https://www.wiz.io/blog/red-agent-snowflake-copilot-cicd-bug)
- **Published:** 2026-08-17
- **Why it matters:** The case shows both sides of AI-assisted security: generated or reviewed code can miss critical regressions while autonomous agents can discover and exploit them within days.

Wiz said its Red Agent autonomously found and exploited a GitHub Actions injection in Snowflake's public connector repository five days after the flaw went live, obtaining credentials that could read internal Jira projects. A merged pull request credited Copilot Autofix as a co-author, and GitHub's AI-assisted review marked the change clear without detecting the vulnerability; Wiz clarified that it is unknown whether AI authored the vulnerable change itself. Snowflake patched the issue and rotated the credential after responsible disclosure, and audit logs reportedly showed no access beyond Wiz's testing.
