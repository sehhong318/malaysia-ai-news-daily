#### 2. Microsoft-led RetroChimera improves AI retrosynthesis planning

- **Why it matters:** More reliable synthesis routes could reduce a major bottleneck between designing promising molecules and producing them for drug or materials research.

Microsoft Research, GSK, Novartis and collaborators developed RetroChimera, which ensembles models with different inductive biases to propose routes from target molecules back to available chemical building blocks. The team still plans real-world discovery evaluations, so benchmark and expert-preference results do not establish laboratory success.
