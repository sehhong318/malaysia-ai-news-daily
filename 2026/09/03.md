#### 1. New York City bans student-facing generative AI through eighth grade for one year

- **Source:** [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/zohran-mamdani-ai-ban-nyc-schools-647f6a968eea0399521b7934418b1aff)
- **Published:** 2026-09-02
- **Why it matters:** Policy in the United States' largest school system could influence how other districts balance child safety and learning benefits from generative AI.

New York City will impose a one-year moratorium on student-facing generative-AI software for pupils through eighth grade and prohibit companion chatbots across all grades. High-school students will instead receive twice-yearly AI-literacy classes, with supervised pilots for selected platforms; teachers may continue using AI for planning and operational work. The city describes this as an assessment period, and critics say implementation and product-safeguard questions remain unresolved.
