#### 6. Korean–English benchmark finds model-safety behaviour varies by language and cultural context

- **Source:** [Information Security Media Group](https://www.bankinfosecurity.com/korean-ai-benchmark-exposes-gaps-in-multilingual-safety-a-32855)
- **Published:** 2026-09-17
- **Why it matters:** Safety evaluations conducted only in English can misrepresent how models behave for users in other languages and cultures.

Scale AI and the Korean AI Safety Institute created ROK-FORTRESS, testing 14 proprietary, open-weight and Korean-focused models with more than 1,200 harmful prompts in English and Korean. Across the tested models, Korean-language prompts grounded in Korean culture produced fewer harmful responses than comparable English prompts, contrary to a simple assumption that English-heavy training guarantees stronger English safeguards. The result is benchmark-specific and does not establish that Korean prompts are universally safer across tasks or deployments.
