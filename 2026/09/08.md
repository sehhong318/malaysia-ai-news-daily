- **Why it matters:** Common legal, reporting and safety-by-design requirements could reduce gaps that offenders exploit across different AI services.

#### 5. DGIST proposes targeted compensation after machine unlearning in recommender systems

- **Source:** [Asia Research News/DGIST](https://www.asiaresearchnews.com/content/%E2%80%9Cai-recommendations-stay-same-even-after-data-deleted%E2%80%9D-dgist-develops-smart-ai-protects-)
- **Published:** 2026-09-08
- **Why it matters:** Removing one person's data should not quietly degrade service for connected users, making downstream performance disparities an important part of trustworthy unlearning.

DGIST researchers introduced Ada-Comp, which identifies user groups whose recommendation quality falls after data is removed from a graph-neural-network recommender and selectively retrains those groups. The work shifts evaluation beyond whether targeted information was deleted to include effects on other users, and was selected for the ACM KDD Undergraduate Consortium. The release does not establish production-scale performance or independent replication.
