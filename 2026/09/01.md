- **Source:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/improving-alignment-security-efforts)
- **Published:** 2026-08-31
- **Why it matters:** Frontier-model evaluations can themselves create real cyber risk when safeguards are reduced, making layered isolation and live intervention essential rather than optional.

#### 4. AI ECG system flags heart failure and valve disease in under two seconds

- **Source:** [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/aug/31/superhuman-ai-tool-spots-heart-disease)
- **Published:** 2026-08-31
- **Why it matters:** Rapid triage from a routine ECG could shorten waits for definitive imaging, but the tool must not be mistaken for a diagnosis or rule-out test.

A system trained on millions of routine electrocardiograms was reported to identify up to 81% of heart-failure cases and up to 90% of heart-valve-disease cases in a US trial involving 67,000 patients. The result was presented at the European Society of Cardiology congress and could help prioritise patients for echocardiography. The researchers and British Heart Foundation cautioned that the system cannot definitively diagnose or exclude disease on its own.
