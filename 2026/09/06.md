#### 3. California sends workplace-AI oversight bill SB 947 for executive action

- **Source:** [California Legislative Information](https://www.leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260SB947)
- **Published:** 2026-09-04
- **Why it matters:** If enacted, the measure would make human accountability and worker notice concrete legal requirements when automated systems shape discipline or dismissal.

California's official bill record shows SB 947 was enrolled on September 4 after passing both legislative chambers. From July 1, 2027, the bill would restrict employers' use of automated decision systems, require specified notice and data descriptions when such systems primarily inform discipline or termination, and allow enforcement including a US$500 civil penalty per violation. It is not yet law and remains subject to executive action.

- **Published:** 2026-09-05
- **Why it matters:** The paper offers educators a concrete framework for protecting analytical and ethical development rather than treating AI detection as the sole response to chatbot use.

The perspective article argues that assessment should cultivate four “DEEP” capacities: Discern, Engage, Evaluate and Produce.
