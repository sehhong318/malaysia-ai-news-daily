#### 3. Clinical team proposes human-backed suicide safeguards for health chatbots

- **Source:** [npj Digital Medicine](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-03288-9)
- **Published:** 2026-09-22
- **Why it matters:** In high-risk care, automated crisis detection without a clear route to trained humans can turn a useful chatbot into a patient-safety hazard.

Researchers describe a three-tier “Safety Router” used in Suzy, a chatbot supporting patients receiving medication treatment for opioid-use disorder: clear danger triggers emergency guidance, ambiguous distress prompts clarification, and low-risk messages continue normally. Trained staff also reviewed conversations twice each weekday and escalated concerning cases using an evidence-based suicide-risk assessment. The authors explicitly call this a pilot design from one clinical deployment that still needs empirical validation elsewhere and say AI-only monitoring is insufficient.
