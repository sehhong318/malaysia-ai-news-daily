- **Published:** 2026-09-17
- **Why it matters:** Repatriation by a large foreign investor group could lift Malaysia's borrowing costs just as sovereign issuance and subsidy pressures are increasing.

#### 5. Budi Diesel subsidy rights can now be transferred to non-family vehicle users

- **Source:** [AutoBuzz Malaysia](https://autobuzz.my/2026/09/17/budi-diesel-subsidy-now-transferable-beyond-immediate-family-members/)
- **Published:** 2026-09-17
- **Why it matters:** The rule better aligns subsidy access with the person actually using a diesel vehicle, particularly in Sabah and Sarawak where shared use is common.

The Finance Ministry has expanded Budi Madani rules so an individual owner may transfer diesel-subsidy eligibility to as many as three Malaysian MyKad holders who actually use the vehicle and qualify for Budi 95; previously, transfers were restricted to immediate family. The change took effect on September 16, does not require an ownership transfer and can be exercised only once, with applications due by the 27th to take effect the following month. Eligible pick-up and jeep owners must first apply for the extra 100-litre quota that brings their monthly allocation to 400 litres.
