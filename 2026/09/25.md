#### 5. Anthropic tests AI agents negotiating in a miniature book market

- **Source:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/project-swap)
- **Published:** 2026-09-24
- **Why it matters:** Delegating transactions to agents depends as much on accurately representing a person's preferences and market rules as on bargaining ability.

In Project Swap, employees across six Anthropic offices briefly described their reading preferences before Claude agents negotiated book exchanges on their behalf.
