- **Published:** 2026-09-28

#### 5. Experiment links misleading AI summaries to distorted eyewitness memory

- **Source:** [Neuroscience News, reporting a Georgetown–University of Washington study](https://neurosciencenews.com/ai-false-memory-llm-31261/)
- **Published:** 2026-09-27
- **Why it matters:** Human review may not correct a faulty automated summary if exposure to the summary changes the reviewer's memory of the original event.

Researchers tested ChatGPT and Gemini on animated traffic incidents and reported that summaries omitted 51.6% of central events on average, including the pedestrian collision in 95% of runs. In a separate experiment, 331 participants watched an incident and later read accurate or misleading summaries; misleading text reduced accurate recall and produced false memories regardless of whether participants were told a human or AI wrote it. The controlled animation setting limits generalisation, and the work presented at AIES needs scrutiny and replication before being applied to real police footage.
