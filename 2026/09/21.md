#### 1. Open-source OCR extracts breast-cancer genomic scores with 97% agreement

- **Source:** [Bioengineer.org, reporting a *Cancer Causes & Control* study](https://bioengineer.org/ai-reads-the-charts-how-open-source-ocr-could-slash-cancer-data-delays/)
- **Published:** 2026-09-21
- **Why it matters:** A low-cost, locally deployable pipeline could shorten the long delay between clinically available genomic results and their use in cancer registries and real-world research.

University of Minnesota researchers tested Tesseract, EasyOCR and a hybrid pipeline on 675 scanned Oncotype DX reports from one US health system. The hybrid achieved 97% agreement with manually abstracted recurrence scores, 0.997 precision and 0.984 F1, compared with 91% agreement for the existing registry abstraction; it processed the set in about 4.9 hours. The retrospective, single-system study covered one standardised report type, and some errors crossed clinically meaningful risk categories, so prospective deployment still requires confidence thresholds and human review.
