### Malaysia News

#### 1. ADB raises Malaysia's 2026 and 2027 growth forecasts

- **Source:** [Malay Mail/Bernama, reporting the Asian Development Bank outlook](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/09/23/adb-raises-malaysias-2026-2027-growth-forecasts-to-49pc-47pc/236278)
- **Published:** 2026-09-23
- **Why it matters:** The upgrade points to semiconductor and data-centre strength, but its downside assumptions show how external conflict, food and energy costs could still weaken household demand.

ADB raised its Malaysia GDP-growth forecasts to 4.9% for 2026 and 4.7% for 2027, from 4.6% and 4.5% in its July outlook, citing the semiconductor upcycle and data-centre investment. It expects inflation to remain at 2.0% in both years while warning that West Asia supply-chain disruption and El Niño-related pressure on local output could slow growth. These are forecasts, not measured outcomes.

#### 2. Malaysia proposes operational framework for security cooperation with China

- **Source:** [New Straits Times/Bernama](https://www.nst.com.my/news/nation/2026/09/1539648/malaysia-china-strengthen-security-cooperation-through-joint-working)
- **Published:** 2026-09-23
- **Why it matters:** Formal terms of reference and working groups could deepen bilateral law-enforcement ties in sensitive areas including intelligence, cyber threats, narcotics and extradition.

Home Minister Saifuddin Nasution Ismail said Malaysia proposed jointly drafting terms of reference for a Security Cooperation Working Group with China, covering intelligence exchange, capacity building, transnational crime, cybersecurity and border management. Malaysia also proposed a dedicated narcotics working group and said it remained committed to resuming negotiations on an extradition treaty first discussed in 2018. These are government proposals and ongoing negotiations, not yet completed agreements.

- **Source:** [Malay Mail/Bernama](https://www.malaymail.com/amp/news/malaysia/2026/09/23/uuca-replacement-bill-expected-to-be-tabled-in-december-says-zambry/236280)

Higher Education Minister Zambry Abd Kadir said the government would try to table the first reading of a bill replacing the Universities and University Colleges Act during Parliament's December sitting. He said stakeholder engagement was continuing, including feedback from a parliamentary special select committee and representatives from both government and opposition. The timing is therefore an indicated target, not a confirmed tabling date, and the draft has not yet completed consultation.

#### 4. Unsubsidised petrol and diesel prices rise for September 24–30

- **Source:** [The Edge Malaysia, citing the Ministry of Finance](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/819111)
- **Published:** 2026-09-23
- **Why it matters:** The widening gap between market and subsidised prices raises the fiscal cost of shielding eligible households and businesses from global fuel disruption.

The Finance Ministry set unsubsidised RON97 at RM5.05 per litre, unsubsidised RON95 at RM4.57 and unsubsidised diesel at RM5.42 for September 24–30, increases of 20 sen for petrol and 15 sen for diesel. Eligible Malaysians will continue paying RM1.99 for RON95 under Budi95, while eligible diesel users pay RM2.10 under Budi Diesel. The ministry attributed elevated market prices to refined-product supply constraints and said domestic supplies remained sufficient.

### AI News

#### 1. AI executives and researchers brief UN Security Council on frontier-system risks

- **Source:** [UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/09/1168414)
- **Published:** 2026-09-23
- **Why it matters:** Frontier-AI safety has moved into the Security Council's international-security agenda, increasing pressure for cross-border oversight rather than company-only safeguards.

The Security Council heard from Yoshua Bengio and the leaders of OpenAI, Anthropic and Hugging Face on AI's benefits and security risks. Bengio argued that loss-of-control dangers are “real and imminent,” cannot be contained by individual states and require international cooperation; UN News also highlighted the Global Digital Compact and the new Independent International Scientific Panel on AI. The severity and timing of future harms remain disputed projections, and the meeting itself did not create binding rules.

#### 2. Sanders and Casar propose permanent superintelligence ban and temporary frontier pause

- **Source:** [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/ai-superintelligence-bernie-sanders-d6a6a2cd8e71d7c95bdc394595aa85d1)
- **Published:** 2026-09-23
- **Why it matters:** The bill marks one of the most sweeping US legislative attempts to restrict frontier development, even though its path through the current Congress is uncertain.

US Senator Bernie Sanders and Representative Greg Casar introduced legislation that would permanently prohibit systems defined as artificial superintelligence and pause the most advanced AI development until federal safety rules are established. It would create a Department of Artificial Intelligence, require federal approval before deployment of advanced systems and allow penalties of up to 20 years in prison in some cases. AP reports that the bill faces long odds in the Republican-controlled Congress, so it is a proposal rather than enacted policy.

#### 5. Tether releases 191.43-billion-token STEM reasoning dataset

- **Source:** [Tether AI Research](https://tether.io/news/tether-releases-genesis-iii-to-train-ai-to-explain-not-just-answer-stem-problems/)
- **Published:** 2026-09-23
- **Why it matters:** Better-curated synthetic data may let smaller local models improve reasoning without relying solely on more parameters and cloud compute.

Tether released QVAC Genesis III, a synthetic corpus of 191.43 billion tokens across 159.6 million documents and 19 STEM areas, using failure analysis and option-level explanations to teach why answers succeed or fail. Tether reports that token-matched training improved ARC-Easy by 28.57 percentage points, ARC-Challenge by 21.35 points and MMLU STEM by 15.03 points versus Cosmopedia-v2; its paper has been accepted at COLM 2026. These benchmark gains are reported by the releasing organisation and require independent replication.

#### 6. NVIDIA publishes open-weight, streaming speaker-diarisation model

- **Source:** [NVIDIA model card on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Nemotron-3-Diarization)
- **Published:** 2026-09-23
- **Why it matters:** An openly available real-time “who spoke when” model can improve meeting transcription, call analytics and multi-speaker voice interfaces without requiring a closed cloud service.

NVIDIA released Nemotron 3 Diarization, an approximately 99-million-parameter open-weight model for streaming and offline speaker diarisation with support for up to eight speakers. The model card says one checkpoint can use input buffers as low as 80 milliseconds, recommends at least 0.32 seconds for low-latency use and permits commercial and non-commercial deployment under the Open Model Data and Weights License 1.1. Deployment quality will still depend on audio conditions, language, speaker overlap and the chosen latency configuration.
