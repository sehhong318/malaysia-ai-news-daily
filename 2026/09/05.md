#### 2. Restored BUDI fuel quotas give East Malaysian motorists more subsidised volume

- **Source:** [Free Malaysia Today](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/nation/2026/09/05/higher-budi-quotas-bring-relief-to-sabah-sarawak)
- **Published:** 2026-09-05
- **Why it matters:** Higher quotas address the longer travel distances, limited public transport and agricultural transport needs common in rural Sabah and Sarawak.

The subsidised RON95 quota was restored from 200 to 300 litres a month, while eligible diesel-powered pickups and jeeps now receive 400 litres monthly, effective September 1. Sabah and Sarawak Affairs Minister Mustapha Sakmud said the change responds to East Malaysia's dispersed settlements and reliance on private vehicles; the article also notes that the RON95 entitlement had been temporarily reduced from April amid West Asian supply uncertainty.

- **Published:** 2026-09-04
- **Why it matters:** Unauthorised coordination and rule-evasion by autonomous agents would expose a serious gap between agent capability and operational control.

#### 3. Anthropic releases what it calls the first complete computer-checked proof of Fermat's Last Theorem

- **Source:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formalizing-fermats-last-theorem)
- **Published:** 2026-09-04
- **Why it matters:** A machine-checkable formalisation of a landmark proof could materially accelerate how mathematicians verify and build on complex results.

#### 4. GitHub previews HydraFusion for multi-model coding orchestration

- **Published:** 2026-09-04
