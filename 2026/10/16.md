#### 1. Conference of Rulers considers prime-minister term limit and AG–prosecutor split

- **Source:** [Bernama](https://www.bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2620958)
- **Published:** 2026-10-15
- **Why it matters:** Consultation with the rulers is an important constitutional step, but neither reform is law until the amendment process is completed.

These were deliberations on proposals, not royal assent or parliamentary passage.

These are IMF projections and regional-policy assessments, not guaranteed outcomes.

#### 3. Malaysia chooses ASEAN diplomacy rather than legal action over haze

- **Source:** [The Straits Times/Bernama](https://www.straitstimes.com/asia/se-asia/malaysia-has-no-plans-to-take-legal-action-or-issue-diplomatic-note-over-haze-says-foreign-minister)
- **Published:** 2026-10-16
- **Why it matters:** The decision defines Malaysia's immediate foreign-policy response to a public-health crisis while leaving questions about accountability and enforcement unresolved.

Foreign Minister Mohamad Hasan said Malaysia would not yet pursue legal enforcement or issue a diplomatic note to Indonesia, preferring leader-level talks and ASEAN mechanisms that preserve bilateral relations. He said officials would coordinate possible firefighting help and Malaysia would submit a non-paper at upcoming ASEAN meetings to strengthen the regional response. This is the government's chosen diplomatic approach, not evidence that the fires or their health effects have been contained.

#### 4. Bank Negara plans API-based regulatory reporting from late 2028

- **Source:** [The Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/822020)
- **Published:** 2026-10-15
- **Why it matters:** Automated, granular reporting could improve financial supervision and open-data quality, but regulated firms face infrastructure, skills and implementation costs.

Bank Negara Malaysia said Project Stream will replace its 2012-era Integrated Statistical System with automated, machine-readable submissions from banks, insurers, takaful operators and payment providers. The first phase, covering deposits, payments and derivatives, is scheduled for late 2028, with later phases extending to credit, balance-of-payments and compliance data. The central bank says industry consultations are broadly supportive, while participants have raised readiness and timetable concerns.

#### 5. Five civil-aviation public-service schemes are abolished after CAAM status change

- **Source:** [Human Resources Online](https://www.humanresourcesonline.net/malaysia-abolishes-five-civil-aviation-service-schemes-effective-this-month)
- **Published:** 2026-10-16
- **Why it matters:** The administrative change ends new appointments under federal schemes for several safety-critical aviation occupations and shifts workforce governance toward CAAM's new statutory status.

A Public Service Department circular abolished schemes for air-traffic control officers, pilot or pilot examiners, aircraft examiners and their assistant roles, effective October 1. It said no new appointments may be made under those schemes following the Civil Aviation Authority of Malaysia's change from a federal statutory body to a remunerated, separated and exempted statutory body. The source does not report dismissals of existing officers or quantify staffing effects.

#### 6. Government studies blacklisting repeat illegal e-waste offenders

- **Source:** [Bernama](https://www.bernama.com/en/general/news.php?id=2621079)
- **Published:** 2026-10-16
- **Why it matters:** A blacklist and battery-lifecycle rules could strengthen environmental enforcement as electronics imports and electric-vehicle battery waste increase, but neither measure is final.

In a parliamentary written reply, the Natural Resources and Environmental Sustainability Ministry said the Department of Environment is studying a blacklist for repeat e-waste offenders while drafting Phase 2(b) amendments to the Environmental Quality Act. Authorities inspected 1,241 containers in 2021–2025, seized 701 containing e-waste and returned 428; the ministry is also developing an ASEAN-first battery-passport standard while a mandatory producer take-back regime for EV batteries remains under study. The reported 13,743.15 tonnes of SW103 battery waste covers all battery types, not only EV batteries.

#### 1. Meta releases an 816-billion-parameter open-weight model for local agentic work

- **Source:** [Meta AI Research](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-spark-open)
- **Published:** 2026-10-15
- **Why it matters:** A frontier-scale model that can run on high-end desktop hardware broadens local deployment and inspection, although its licence and hardware requirements limit how open and accessible it is in practice.

Meta released Muse Spark Open, a multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 816 billion total parameters, 42 billion active parameters and a one-million-token context window, aimed at coding and multi-step agent tasks. Meta says it can run across two Apple M5 Ultra Mac Studios and made weights available through several cloud and model platforms under a Muse licence with limited commercial terms. Capability and safety results are vendor-reported, and Meta says a full evaluation report will follow.

#### 3. IRIS model reconstructs cell-signalling histories across unseen cell types

- **Source:** [MIT News](https://news.mit.edu/2026/ai-model-decodes-language-cells-use-communicate-1015)
- **Published:** 2026-10-15
- **Why it matters:** Transferable signalling fingerprints could accelerate stem-cell engineering and disease models while reducing the need to experimentally map every pathway in every cell type.

MIT and Whitehead Institute researchers developed IRIS, a neural-network model trained on human embryonic stem-cell responses to combinations of six signalling pathways. It inferred pathway activity in previously unseen cell types and correctly predicted a lung-development signal later tested in mouse embryos; the open-access study appeared in Nature Methods on September 8. The results are research-stage demonstrations and do not yet establish clinical effectiveness in human regenerative therapies.

- **Source:** [Chemical & Engineering News](https://cen.acs.org/education/graduate-education/generative-ai-use-phd-dissertations/104/web/2026/10)
- **Published:** 2026-10-15
- **Why it matters:** Rapid AI adoption in doctoral writing raises questions about disclosure, authorship and whether students are still developing independent scientific communication skills.

Chemical & Engineering News reports that a National Bureau of Economic Research working paper estimates just over 29% of US STEM PhD dissertations submitted through May 2026 contain AI-generated writing, up from 1.7% in 2023. The result indicates a sharp increase in detectable use, but it comes from a working paper and text-classification methodology rather than verified author admissions. It therefore should not be read as proof of misconduct or fully AI-written dissertations.
