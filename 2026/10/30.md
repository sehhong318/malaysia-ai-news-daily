#### 1. Government signs confidence-and-supply agreement with 13 opposition MPs

- **Source:** [Malaysiakini](https://www.malaysiakini.com/news/888115-govt-signs-csa-with-bersatu-mps-7-other-opposition-lawmakers)
- **Published:** 2026-10-29
- **Why it matters:** The agreement strengthens the government's ability to pass budgets and survive confidence votes while attaching parliamentary support to reform and equal-allocation commitments.

The government signed a confidence-and-supply agreement with six Bersatu MPs led by party president Muhyiddin Yassin and seven other opposition MPs, including Muar MP Syed Saddiq Syed Abdul Rahman. Malaysiakini reported that the 13 agreed to support Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim's administration through the current parliamentary term and back Budget 2027; in return, they sought equal constituency-development funds and action on a prime-ministerial term limit and separation of the attorney-general and public prosecutor roles. The signed agreement materially updates the previous evening's report, when participation and terms were still unconfirmed; the requested reforms remain commitments rather than enacted law.

#### 2. Dewan Rakyat passes Budget 2027 at policy stage

- **Source:** [The Star/Bernama](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/10/29/dewan-rakyat-passes-supply-bill-2027-at-policy-stage)
- **Published:** 2026-10-29
- **Why it matters:** Policy-stage approval is the budget's first major parliamentary test, but detailed votes and Senate consideration remain before it becomes law.

The Dewan Rakyat passed the Supply Bill 2027 at policy stage by majority voice vote after debate that began on October 12. The bill provides RM470 billion for 2027—RM338.2 billion in operating expenditure, RM81 billion in development expenditure and RM50.8 billion for government-linked investment companies and statutory bodies—according to the report. Passage at this stage does not complete the legislative process; ministry-level committee debate was scheduled to continue from November 2 to 26.

#### 1. OpenAI updates its Model Spec around five core values

- **Source:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/updated-model-spec)
- **Published:** 2026-10-29
- **Why it matters:** A public behaviour specification provides a reference for how a frontier-model developer resolves conflicts among user intent, safety, human agency and system-level constraints.

OpenAI published an updated Model Spec organised around five core values intended to guide more capable systems toward sound judgment, human agency and faithfulness to human intent. #### 3. Mistral releases weights for trillion-parameter Large 4

- **Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/deepgram-acquires-pipecat-maker-daily-to-bolster-its-voice-ai-infrastructure/)
- **Published:** 2026-10-29
- **Why it matters:** Combining speech models with a widely used real-time orchestration framework could consolidate the voice-agent stack, making the continuing neutrality of Pipecat consequential for developers.

The openness commitment is prospective and will need to be assessed against post-acquisition governance and interoperability.
