#### 1. Major AI providers back a shared robots.txt training-preference signal

- **Source:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/helping-websites-express-ai-training-preferences)
- **Published:** 2026-10-30
- **Why it matters:** A cross-provider opt-out mechanism could reduce the burden on publishers that currently maintain crawler-specific rules, though it remains voluntary and is not yet an adopted Internet standard.

OpenAI said it will honour a shared robots.txt signal that lets website operators express one AI-training preference to participating providers, alongside its existing crawler-specific controls. The underlying Internet-Draft defines a `__usage_ai-training` policy token and OpenAI plans implementation in coming months; Google, Microsoft and Anthropic have also been reported as supporters. An Internet-Draft has no formal IETF-standard status, and the mechanism communicates a preference rather than technically preventing collection or guaranteeing compliance.
