- **Why it matters:** Enforcing permissions outside an AI agent could limit the damage from prompt manipulation or faulty reasoning, but real deployments must show that safeguards block dangerous actions without disabling useful work.

- **Published:** 2026-10-04
- **Why it matters:** An inexpensive imaging-based marker could support higher-throughput screening of interventions, but classification of cellular patterns is not proof of rejuvenation or improved organism health.

The *Aging Cell* study is preclinical, and neither a younger classification nor performance on mouse cells demonstrates restored function or effectiveness in humans.
