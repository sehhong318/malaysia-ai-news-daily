- **Why it matters:** A reusable model and public training resources could lower the labelled-data burden for lunar research and mission planning, especially around polar ice.

Results are team-reported, some test sets are small, and the model is not a substitute for physical measurements or reliable absolute positioning.

#### 4. Regularised harness optimisation improves AI-agent generalisation in reported tests

- **Published:** 2026-10-04

Researchers from Google Cloud AI Research and universities introduced Regularized Recursive Self-Improvement of Agent Harnesses, which limits the number of edits, shrinks edit budgets over time and rejects benchmark-specific hardcoding.
