#### 3. Suhakam chairman calls for a mandatory government “respond and explain” mechanism

- **Source:** [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/10/05/suhakam-doesnt-need-prosecutorial-powers-but-govt-must-answer-its-findings-says-chairman/237687)
- **Published:** 2026-10-05
- **Why it matters:** Requiring reasoned, time-bound responses could strengthen accountability for human-rights findings without turning Suhakam into a prosecutor or court.

Suhakam chairman Mohd Hishamudin Md Yunus said the commission does not need prosecutorial powers but authorities should have to state in writing whether they accept, partly accept or reject its recommendations, explain rejections and report implementation progress. The proposal is the chairman's institutional-reform position and has not been adopted by Parliament or the government.

#### 2. Altman argues AI's benefits justify accepting some harms

#### 4. NASA and IBM release an open-source lunar foundation model

- **Source:** [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/nasa-and-ibms-open-source-lunar-model-turns-17-years-of-orbiter-data-into-a-foundation-for-lunar-science/)
- **Published:** 2026-10-04
- **Why it matters:** A reusable model and public training resources could lower the labelled-data burden for lunar research and mission planning, especially around polar ice.

The NASA-IBM Lunar Foundation Model was trained from scratch on nearly two million multimodal tile bundles drawn largely from 17 years of Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter observations, with additional mission data. The team reports up to 22% lower error for polar-ice prediction and almost 19% improvement over a selected baseline for coarse crater detection; model weights, code and datasets were released publicly. Results are team-reported, some test sets are small, and the model is not a substitute for physical measurements or reliable absolute positioning.
