The Home Ministry said Malaysia retained Tier 2 in the US State Department's 2026 Trafficking in Persons Report. #### 2. Government discloses 19 DNAA cases involving 12 politicians since 2010

- **Source:** [The Vibes](https://www.thevibes.com/index.php/articles/news/128101/twelve-politicians-involved-in-19-cases-granted-dnaa-since-2010)
- **Published:** 2026-10-09
- **Why it matters:** The parliamentary disclosure provides a concrete measure for scrutiny of prosecutorial discretion and equal treatment in politically sensitive criminal cases.

Law and Institutional Reform Minister Azalina Othman Said said in a written parliamentary reply that High Courts and subordinate courts issued discharges not amounting to acquittal in 19 cases involving 12 politicians from 2010 through July 2026. She said the figures came from Chief Registrar records and that the Attorney-General's Chambers holds case-specific prosecution records. A DNAA is not an acquittal and permits a later charge on the same offence; the aggregate count alone does not establish that any individual decision was improper.

The four outstanding measures and the wider action matrix are not yet completed outcomes.
