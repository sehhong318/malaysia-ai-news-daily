- **Published:** 2026-10-25

- **Why it matters:** AI may improve longer-range storm tracks and disaster preparation, but its weaker estimates of intensity and speed argue for hybrid forecasting rather than replacing physical models.

The contemporaneous forecast for Typhoon Champi remained uncertain, so one storm's projected path was not treated as a completed validation.
