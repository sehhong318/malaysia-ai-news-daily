#### 5. Study finds 97.7% of matched AI incidents lacked substantive US securities disclosure

- **Published:** 2026-10-21
- **Why it matters:** Generic AI-risk language may leave investors unable to reconstruct companies' realised incident records from regulatory filings.

The estimates depend on the incident database and matching method and do not establish that any omitted event was legally material or that an issuer violated disclosure rules.
