- **Published:** 2026-10-20

#### 4. Malaysia considers temporary crude-palm-oil export-tax waiver as stocks reach 3.5 million tonnes

- **Source:** [New Straits Times](https://www.nst.com.my/business/economy/2026/10/1560190/malaysia-mulls-cpo-tax-waiver-palm-oil-stocks-hit-record-35mil)
- **Published:** 2026-10-21
- **Why it matters:** A waiver could accelerate exports and support producer prices, but it would trade away revenue and could weaken Malaysian refiners' feedstock advantage.

The government is considering waiving the 10% crude palm oil export tax after inventories reached 3.5 million tonnes, with the Plantation and Commodities Ministry expected to consult industry. An analyst estimated the tax at about RM450 per tonne and the fiscal cost at roughly RM450 million for every million tonnes exported tax-free, while warning that Indonesian palm oil remains RM700–RM900 per tonne cheaper; no waiver has yet been adopted.

- **Source:** [New Straits Times](https://www.nst.com.my/business/economy/2026/10/1560192/malaysia-posts-rm157tril-exports-nine-months-growth-may-cool)

- **Why it matters:** The record shows strong external demand—especially for electronics linked to AI investment—while concentration in the technology cycle leaves Malaysia exposed to a slowdown.

Electrical and electronics products contributed RM253.88 billion of the export increase; economists cautioned that volume and unit-value data are still needed to judge underlying strength and that growth may moderate from the high base.

### AI News

#### 1. Google open-sources TorchTPU for native PyTorch workloads on its accelerators

- **Source:** [Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/ai-infrastructure/torchtpu-is-now-open-source-run-native-pytorch-on-tpus)
- **Published:** 2026-10-21
- **Why it matters:** Native PyTorch support reduces software lock-in around GPU-centric tooling and could make Google's TPUs a more accessible alternative for training and inference.

Google released TorchTPU on GitHub after co-developing it with Meta's PyTorch team, saying existing PyTorch workloads can run on TPUs with minimal code changes. The stack supports tools including vLLM, SGLang, llm-d, TorchTitan, Ray and Hugging Face libraries; production performance and portability claims still depend on workloads and independent testing.

#### 2. IBM and partners release llm-d 1.0 for production distributed inference

- **Source:** [IBM Research](https://research.ibm.com/blog/llmd-1-0-is-ready-for-production-inference-serving)
- **Published:** 2026-10-20
- **Why it matters:** Operational features for shared, distributed serving address the recurring inference cost and reliability problems that arise after models leave benchmark environments.

IBM announced version 1.0 of the Kubernetes-native, open-source llm-d serving network, maintained with Red Hat, Google and a wider contributor community. The release adds stable APIs, multi-tenant quality-of-service controls, zero-downtime operations and agent-aware routing around techniques such as prefix-cache reuse and prefill/decode disaggregation; cited cost and cache-reuse gains are project demonstrations rather than universal benchmarks.

#### 3. DeepSeek releases the production-derived DeepInfer framework under Apache 2.0

- **Source:** [DeepSeek on GitHub](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepInfer)
- **Published:** 2026-10-20
- **Why it matters:** Publishing the inference code behind a major model provider gives operators another open stack to inspect and optimise across Nvidia GPUs and Huawei accelerators.

DeepSeek released DeepInfer's core offline-inference components, including optimised kernels, asynchronous scheduling, parallelism, deterministic sampling and structured outputs for V4.1-Flash. The repository supports Nvidia SM100 GPUs and Huawei Ascend 950 NPUs, but explicitly omits some components required for a complete serving service, including a KV-cache system and cross-replica load balancing.

- **Source:** [Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/unifying-edge-ai-how-coral-npus-open-standard-brings-portable-energy-viable-intelligence-from-model-to-silicon/)
- **Published:** 2026-10-20
- **Why it matters:** An open architecture and toolchain could reduce fragmentation in low-power edge AI, where models otherwise need chip-specific kernels and compilers.

It also announced Synaptics' 400-GOPS Astra SR200 microcontrollers and new development boards; performance and power figures are developer and vendor targets pending broader independent validation.

#### 5. Emergency-triage study finds stigmatizing clinical language can alter model priorities

- **Source:** [UT Southwestern via Newswise](https://www.newswise.com/articles/artificial-intelligence-enabled-triage-tools-may-reflect-human-biases)
- **Published:** 2026-10-20
- **Why it matters:** Models used to allocate urgent care may reproduce documentation bias even when the added language is unrelated to immediate medical severity.

Researchers paired emergency-department records with similar initial severity and asked three open-source language models which patients were likelier to deteriorate. Adding descriptions associated with frequent visits, psychiatric emergencies or substance use sometimes caused models to deprioritise patients who later worsened, with nearly 10% incorrectly downgraded in some tests; this retrospective model evaluation does not establish clinical deployment outcomes.

#### 6. EA-VAE method improves uncertainty estimates under common image distortions

- **Source:** [HUN-REN](https://hun-ren.hu/research_news/the-ability-to-doubt-could-make-artificial-intelligence-more-reliable-110404)
- **Published:** 2026-10-20
- **Why it matters:** Better-calibrated uncertainty can help image systems defer ambiguous or out-of-distribution cases instead of making confidently wrong decisions.

A Hungarian–Argentinian team added a global scaling latent variable to a variational autoencoder so it can separate object-related changes from factors such as contrast or brightness. HUN-REN reports that the resulting EA-VAE produced more consistent uncertainty estimates than standard VAEs across several image datasets and corruption conditions; potential medical-imaging benefits remain prospective rather than clinically validated.

#### 7. CSU combines short- and medium-range AI systems for earlier hail forecasts

- **Source:** [Colorado State University](https://engr.source.colostate.edu/csu-uses-artificial-intelligence-to-improve-hail-forecasting/)
- **Published:** 2026-10-20
- **Why it matters:** Extending useful hail warnings from minutes toward hours or days could improve safety, agricultural preparation and insurance-risk management.

Colorado State University researchers are linking an Nvidia-assisted regional nowcasting system with a machine-learning model trained on nine years of US severe-weather observations. The short-range Hail-StormScope component is designed for two-to-three-hour lead times, while the other system estimates one-to-eight-day probabilities; the combined approach is still under development, with new ground sensors planned for validation in spring 2027.
