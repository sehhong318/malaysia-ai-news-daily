- **Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/reflection-debuts-beam-a-open-weight-ai-model-to-rival-chinese-models-at-lower-compute-cost/)
- **Published:** 2026-10-05
- **Why it matters:** A large US-developed open-weight reasoning model could broaden alternatives to leading Chinese open models, but independent evaluation is needed before accepting its efficiency and capability claims.

Reflection AI unveiled Beam, a text-only mixture-of-experts model with 501 billion total parameters, 23 billion active parameters and a one-million-token context window. #### 6. Google tests terrestrial AI chips aboard a low-Earth-orbit satellite

- **Source:** [Tech Wire Asia](https://techwireasia.com/2026/10/google-ai-chips-space-orbital-data-centres/)
- **Published:** 2026-10-05
- **Why it matters:** The experiment provides real hardware evidence for or against orbital computing proposals that promise abundant solar power but face radiation, cooling and high-bandwidth networking constraints.

Google placed four Trillium-generation tensor processing units on a prototype satellite launched October 1 for Project Suncatcher and said contact had been established and the spacecraft was operating as expected. The mission is testing whether terrestrial AI processors can survive launch and radiation, shed heat in a vacuum and eventually exchange enough data across satellites to act as a larger system. It is an experiment, not a functioning orbital data centre or evidence that the economics and environmental trade-offs are favourable.
