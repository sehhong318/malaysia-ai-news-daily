#### 1. Malaysia will question Myanmar over reports that returnees remain in military custody

- **Source:** [The Edge Malaysia/Reuters](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/823326)
- **Published:** 2026-10-27
- **Why it matters:** The reported detention directly tests Myanmar's assurance of safe and dignified returns and Malaysia's responsibility not to expose returnees to serious harm.

Foreign Minister Mohamad Hasan told Parliament that Malaysia's chargé d'affaires would investigate reports that hundreds of people recently repatriated from Malaysian detention centres remain in military custody in Myanmar. Malaysia returned about 1,500 Myanmar nationals in late September under what it called voluntary repatriation, while the UN and rights groups warned that safe return cannot be guaranteed amid Myanmar's civil war. The detentions are attributed to media reports and have not been independently confirmed by Malaysia; Mohamad said Kuala Lumpur would seek an explanation and insist that Myanmar honour its assurances.

#### 6. Malaysia may extend existing coal plants while maintaining its ban on new ones

- **Source:** [The Straits Times/Bloomberg](https://www.straitstimes.com/asia/se-asia/malaysia-says-extending-life-of-coal-plants-remains-an-option-if-needed)
- **Published:** 2026-10-26
- **Why it matters:** Keeping coal units online longer could improve near-term grid reliability but complicate Malaysia's clean-energy transition and emissions trajectory.

Deputy Prime Minister and energy-transition minister Fadillah Yusof said Malaysia may extend the operating life of existing coal-fired plants if energy security requires it, while continuing to reject new coal facilities. He cited disrupted Middle East energy flows and rising electricity demand, including from data centres; Malaysia's last coal station is currently scheduled to retire in 2044, and the country targets a 70% clean-energy electricity mix by 2050. This is an option under consideration, not an approved extension for any named plant, and Fadillah said co-firing with other fuels could be considered.

#### 3. Cloudflare discloses cross-customer data exposure in its GLM-5.3 service

- **Source:** [Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/kv-cache-exposure-remediation/)
- **Published:** 2026-10-26
- **Why it matters:** The incident shows that shared inference infrastructure can leak customer data through cache-lifecycle errors even without a model jailbreak or deliberate attack.

Cloudflare said 155 GLM-5.3 Workers AI responses between August 27 and September 9 contained data from other customers' preceding requests when prompts exceeded 681,280 tokens. It attributed the exposure to a mismatch between the advertised context window and available KV-cache capacity plus an SGLang rejection-path bug that allowed stale cache pages to be used; the model was taken offline, reconfigured, patched and restored on September 9. Cloudflare says it found no evidence of deliberate exploitation and that no other Workers AI model was affected, but those conclusions are based on its investigation.

#### 5. Etsy tightens rules and detection for AI-generated marketplace listings

- **Source:** [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1522733284810)
- **Published:** 2026-10-22
- **Why it matters:** The policy is a large-marketplace test of whether disclosure, authentic product images and human creative involvement can preserve buyer trust without banning AI-assisted work outright.

Etsy's updated seller guidance bans AI-generated functional guides such as patterns, recipes and instructions, requires at least one unedited photo of the real item in a real setting, and prohibits bot-generated listings without human involvement. AI-generated art remains permitted when a seller has a clear creative role and discloses AI use; Etsy says it will flag suspected generated images during listing, expand detection to existing listings and add buyer reporting. Detection accuracy and enforcement consistency remain unproven, and the buyer reports will initially inform Etsy rather than automatically remove listings.
