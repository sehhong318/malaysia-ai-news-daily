#### 1. Machine-learning correction framework improves two-to-six-week weather forecasts

- **Source:** [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/story/new-ai-framework-bridges-a-critical-gap-in-mid-range-weather-forecasts/)
- **Published:** 2026-10-17
- **Why it matters:** More reliable sub-seasonal forecasts could improve farming, water, energy and disaster planning without requiring forecasters to replace their existing models.

Microsoft and external researchers developed Probabilistic Bias Correction, a lightweight machine-learning system that learns systematic errors in existing weather models. Microsoft says the method improved 98% of precipitation, 92% of temperature and 91% of pressure targets for an operationally debiased ECMWF model and doubled the accuracy of ECMWF's AI Forecasting System; a PBC-based entry also led the first season of ECMWF's AI Weather Quest. These results come from the researchers and competition setting, while pilot use in Kenya is still being evaluated in practice.
