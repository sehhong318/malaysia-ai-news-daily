#### 2. Claude traces the unexplained seeds behind widely used NIST elliptic curves

- **Source:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/ecdsa-curve-origins)
- **Published:** 2026-10-16
- **Why it matters:** Reconstructing how opaque cryptographic parameters were generated reduces one longstanding source of suspicion and demonstrates a concrete AI-assisted research workflow.

Anthropic says an internal Claude research model determined in under half an hour that the published seeds for five NIST prime elliptic curves came from the C library's `lrand48` generator initialized with Unix timestamps, rather than from a rumoured hashed phrase. Researchers then reproduced the result and found related origins for several ANSI curves and some base points, while B-163 and several NIST base points remain unexplained. Anthropic stresses that this is not a cryptographic break or new attack and argues that the simple reproducible process makes deliberate backdoors less plausible; the account and timing are company-reported.
