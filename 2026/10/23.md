- **Source:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formal-verification)
- **Published:** 2026-10-22
- **Why it matters:** Machine-checked proofs at this scale could make formal verification practical beyond a small set of safety-critical systems, although the guarantees are narrower than full software correctness.

Anthropic and Theorem said Claude patched discovered bugs and generated Rocq proofs covering 239 OSS-Fuzz entry points across 123 libraries, amounting to about 180 million proof lines checked by the Rocq kernel. The result does not prove the libraries bug-free outside those specifications and assumptions.
