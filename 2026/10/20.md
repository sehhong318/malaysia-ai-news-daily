### AI News

#### 1. Realistic medical-AI benchmarks find agents complete less than half of end-to-end work

- **Source:** [Stanford HAI](https://hai.stanford.edu/news/measuring-what-medical-ai-can-actually-do)
- **Published:** 2026-10-19
- **Why it matters:** The gap between licensing-exam performance and multi-step clinical work cautions against treating medical knowledge scores as evidence that agents can safely operate health-system workflows.

Stanford researchers summarised three years of work on MedHELM, PhysicianBench and HealthAdminBench, moving from individual clinical questions to tasks inside an electronic-health-record environment. They report that leading agents complete less than half of realistic work and often lose track of information across steps, documents and systems; these are benchmark results, not clinical-deployment outcomes.

#### 2. Mathematicians assess an AI-generated proof of the quasi-Riemann hypothesis

- **Source:** [Quanta Magazine](https://www.quantamagazine.org/ai-proved-the-quasi-riemann-hypothesis-what-happens-now-20261019/)
- **Published:** 2026-10-19
- **Why it matters:** If the proof withstands specialist scrutiny, it would be a major AI-assisted mathematics result while still falling short of resolving the full Riemann hypothesis.

Quanta reports that one of 377 results announced by OpenAI on October 6 claims to settle the weaker quasi-Riemann hypothesis through a synthesis of established number-theory ideas. Named specialists described the result as highly significant and are analysing its implications; the claim concerns quasi-RH, not the 166-year-old Riemann hypothesis itself, and continued expert review remains important.

#### 3. OpenCode breach exposes API keys and triggers unauthorised AI-service charges

- **Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/19/hackers-hit-ai-coding-tool-opencode-running-up-charges-on-users-ai-accounts/)
- **Published:** 2026-10-19
- **Why it matters:** Centralised storage of model-provider keys can turn one coding-service breach into costs and exposure across several AI accounts.

- **Source:** [Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/clairic-extraction-model)
- **Published:** 2026-10-19
- **Why it matters:** A specialised single-pass model could make repetitive structured extraction cheaper and more predictable than routing every task through a general-purpose LLM.

The company says the model returns confidence information and typically responds in under a second without generating reasoning tokens; its accuracy, latency and cost advantages are developer-reported and still need independent evaluation.
