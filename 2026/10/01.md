#### 1. Parliamentary Services Act takes effect, restoring a dedicated service after 34 years

- **Source:** [Malay Mail/Bernama](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/10/01/malaysia-restores-parliamentary-service-after-34-years-as-new-law-takes-effect/237189)
- **Published:** 2026-10-01
- **Why it matters:** Separate control over Parliament's administration and personnel is intended to strengthen legislative autonomy from the executive and Malaysia's institutional checks and balances.

The Parliamentary Services Act 2025 (Act 869) and related Federal Constitution amendments came into force on October 1. Law and Institutional Reform Minister Azalina Othman Said said the law restores a dedicated parliamentary service with greater independence in administration, human resources and internal affairs, 34 years after the previous act was repealed; the bill had passed both houses and received royal assent in 2025. Its practical effect will depend on how the new service is staffed, governed and insulated in operation, not only on commencement of the statute.

- **Published:** 2026-10-01

#### 4. Malaysia's manufacturing PMI slips marginally into contraction

- **Source:** [The Star, reporting S&P Global data](https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2026/10/01/malaysia-manufacturing-pmi-slips-into-contraction-in-september)
- **Published:** 2026-10-01
- **Why it matters:** Softer orders and output signal cooling factory momentum, while continued hiring and easing price pressure make the reading more mixed than a broad industrial downturn.

The S&P Global Malaysia Manufacturing PMI fell to 49.9 in September from 50.2 in August, ending three months of improving operating conditions and moving just below the 50 no-change threshold. New orders fell for the first time in four months and output declined for a second month, but employment increased at its fastest pace since April and input-price inflation eased for a fifth month. S&P said the historical PMI relationship still points to solid third-quarter economic expansion; that is a survey-based inference rather than official GDP data.

#### 3. OpenAI says it disrupted an alleged adversarial-distillation campaign linked partly to Moonshot AI

- **Source:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/01/openai-chinas-moonshot-ai-kimi.html)
- **Published:** 2026-10-01
- **Why it matters:** Attempts to reproduce protected model reasoning could reduce the cost of building rival systems while complicating safety controls, intellectual-property enforcement and international AI competition.

OpenAI said operators used manipulated model interactions to try to extract protected reasoning, with activity surging to 16,000 requests from more than 4,000 users over two days and related activity spanning more than 15,000 accounts.
