- **Published:** 2026-10-11
- **Why it matters:** The closure disrupts a vital Kelantan road link and highlights the need to establish the ground failure's cause before temporary access is treated as safe.

#### 6. Industrial production grows 5% in August as manufacturing offsets mining contraction

- **Source:** [KLSE Screener/The Edge Malaysia](https://www.klsescreener.com/v2/news/view/1806005/Malaysia_s_IPI_expands_5_in_August_manufacturing_sales_hit_RM185_bil)
- **Published:** 2026-10-09
- **Why it matters:** Stronger manufacturing and electricity output support the growth outlook, but the mining decline shows that the expansion is not broad-based across sectors.

The Department of Statistics Malaysia said the industrial production index rose 5% year on year in August, accelerating from 4.7% in July, while manufacturing sales increased 9.9% to RM185 billion. Manufacturing output grew 7.4% and electricity 9%, but mining contracted 7.4%; on a monthly basis, total industrial production rebounded 2.6%. The figures describe August output and do not by themselves establish that the pace continued into October.
