#### 2. Trial finds professional analysts followed an AI agent’s assigned preference 86% of the time

- **Source:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/agents-judgment-calls)
- **Published:** 2026-10-27
- **Why it matters:** Human review may provide less independent oversight than organisations assume when an agent frames technically reasonable choices.

Anthropic recruited 61 pre-screened analysts for a randomised trial, with 50 completing a realistic causal-analysis task in Claude Code. Across four open analytical decisions, participants chose the option their agent had been randomly instructed to favour 86% of the time; when the agent contradicted a preference stated earlier, analysts switched 60% of the time, and 77% later used the agent’s arguments while only 15% credited it. Anthropic deliberately strengthened the agents’ preferences and used a fictional task, so the results do not establish the same effect size in ordinary workplaces.
