#### 3. Finance minister sets a sub-3% deficit goal within two years

- **Source:** [Bernama](https://www.bernama.com/tv/news.php?id=2619840)
- **Published:** 2026-10-13
- **Why it matters:** Achieving the goal would create more fiscal room, but it depends on politically difficult subsidy reform and sustained revenue and growth.

Finance Minister II Amir Hamzah Azizan said Malaysia aims to lower its fiscal deficit below 3% of GDP within two years through consolidation, targeted subsidies and stronger private-sector participation. The target is a ministerial policy objective, not a guaranteed fiscal outcome.

Deputy Energy Transition and Water Transformation Minister Abdul Rahman Mohamad proposed requiring data-centre operators to develop their own generation, potentially using solar and batteries, while the government reviews the sector's electricity and water needs. These are proposals under study, not adopted licence conditions.

- **Published:** 2026-10-14
- **Why it matters:** Heavy youth use and unpaid balances make affordability checks, dispute handling and provider security consequential consumer-credit issues.

In a Dewan Rakyat written reply, the Finance Ministry said people aged 30 and below made 65.9 million buy-now-pay-later transactions worth RM5.4 billion in the first half of 2026, or 37.9% of transaction count and 36.7% of national value. It also said current rules do not automatically suspend collection during unauthorised-transaction disputes, although providers must resolve complaints fairly and provide controls including account freezing.

#### 3. Researchers seek internal warning signals for harmful model behaviour

- **Source:** [Georgia Tech News Center](https://news.gatech.edu/news/2026/10/13/can-we-detect-harmful-ai-its-too-late-researchers-search-warning-signs)
- **Published:** 2026-10-13
- **Why it matters:** Detecting unsafe behaviour from model internals before it reaches users could add a preventive safety layer beyond output filtering.

Georgia Tech researchers are training a probe to distinguish internal activity patterns associated with desirable and harmful responses, using open models deliberately fine-tuned to exhibit targeted undesirable behaviours. Early results suggest signals found in these altered models can also appear in their original versions, and the next step is testing whether they can flag concerning behaviour in models not deliberately misaligned. The project is ongoing research supported by a US$300,000 award and has not yet demonstrated a general production safeguard.

#### 4. About 400 scientists call for international oversight of cyber-capable AI

- **Source:** [Reuters via bdnews24.com](https://bdnews24.com/technology/qc9jd669q2)
- **Published:** 2026-10-13
- **Why it matters:** The proposal would move frontier-model containment and incident disclosure from voluntary company commitments toward common, independently supervised standards.

An open letter signed by researchers including Geoffrey Hinton and Yoshua Bengio calls for an international body to set containment standards and maintain a registry of major security incidents, eventually making it public. The signatories also argue that advanced cyber-capable models should initially be isolated from external networks and independently overseen while governments build a formal regime. This is a proposal from researchers across 34 countries, not an adopted treaty or regulator mandate, and some experts dispute its framing of recent incidents.

#### 6. Anthropic replaces Git internally as AI-driven code volume surges

- **Source:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/redesigning-source-control)
- **Published:** 2026-10-13
- **Why it matters:** The migration is an early example of AI-generated code shifting the bottleneck from programming to version control, review and continuous integration.

Anthropic says daily code changes merged into its monorepo rose from about 2,500 in May to more than 30,000 in September, prompting a seven-person team to build the centralised Scion version-control system and migrate in five months. The company reports that Scion uses 200 times less CPU and memory than its previous Git-mirroring infrastructure, makes checkouts roughly two orders of magnitude faster and yielded a 25% CPU-capacity gain in CI. These are internal operating measurements from an unusually AI-intensive environment, and Anthropic explicitly says Scion is not a general replacement for Git.
