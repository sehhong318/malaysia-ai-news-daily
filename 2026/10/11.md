- **Published:** 2026-10-10
- **Why it matters:** The proposal shifts safety from trusting model behaviour toward auditable containment and a human ability to interrupt consequential agent actions.

#### 4. New benchmark tests whether LLM reviewers can detect planted errors in papers

- **Source:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11087)
- **Published:** 2026-10-08
- **Why it matters:** Error-detection benchmarks are more relevant to scientific quality control than simply measuring whether an AI review resembles a human-written one.

Researchers introduced a contradiction benchmark containing 1,164 synthetically inserted errors across 257 papers and a Multi-Layered Review system that reads supporting material before generating a critique.
