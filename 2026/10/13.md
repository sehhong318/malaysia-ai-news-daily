#### 2. Zahid judicial-review challenge is put back pending Federal Court appeal

- **Source:** [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/10/12/high-court-fixes-june-14-2027-for-bar-bid-to-revive-zahid-graft-case/238822)
- **Published:** 2026-10-12
- **Why it matters:** The delay extends uncertainty over judicial review of the prosecution's decision to discontinue a corruption case involving the deputy prime minister.

The Kuala Lumpur High Court fixed June 14, 2027 for case management of the Malaysian Bar's challenge to the prosecution's discontinuance of Ahmad Zahid Hamidi's Yayasan Akalbudi case. The parties are waiting for a Federal Court appeal after the apex court on October 8 allowed Zahid and the attorney general to challenge the appellate ruling that permitted the Bar's judicial review. The new date is a procedural development, not a ruling on the lawfulness of the discharge not amounting to an acquittal or on Zahid's guilt.

#### 3. Cabinet-backed proposal would let criminal courts compensate victims of impaired drivers

- **Source:** [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/10/12/ministry-drafting-amendments-on-dui-accident-victim-compensation-with-agc-says-loke)
- **Published:** 2026-10-12
- **Why it matters:** Combining compensation with criminal proceedings could reduce the cost and delay victims face when pursuing a separate civil suit.

Transport Minister Anthony Loke said the Cabinet has approved the policy in principle and his ministry is drafting Road Transport Act 1987 amendments with the Attorney General's Chambers. The proposal would allow a judge hearing a criminal case against a driver impaired by alcohol or drugs to award compensation to victims; Loke said the government is targeting the first parliamentary sitting of 2027. Technical details remain under development, and the amendment has not yet been tabled or enacted.

#### 3. Intel details a high-memory D-series inference accelerator

- **Source:** [Intel](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/newsroom/news/artificial-intelligence/intel-accelerates-open-infrastructure-agentic-ai-era.html)
- **Published:** 2026-10-12
- **Why it matters:** A standard PCIe accelerator with large, lower-cost memory could broaden hardware options for long-context and multi-tenant inference beyond today's leading GPU platforms.

These are announced specifications and intended capabilities; pricing, broad availability and independent performance results were not provided in the source.

#### 4. Study finds periodic retrieval weakness in compressed long-context models

- **Source:** [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/10/12/study-finds-reliability-issue-in-deepseek-v4-models/)
- **Published:** 2026-10-12
- **Why it matters:** Memory-saving attention can reduce inference cost but may introduce predictable blind spots, complicating use of very long contexts in high-stakes applications.

Researchers affiliated with ByteDance Seed, Princeton, Stanford and UC Berkeley reported “phase sensitivity” in chunk-compressed key-value caches: at roughly 128,000 tokens, retrieval accuracy in some DeepSeek V4 models varied by as much as 40 percentage points depending on the target's position. Their smaller-model experiments found similar periodic sensitivity across tested chunk-compression methods but not full attention. The late-September paper has not been peer reviewed, and the result should not be generalized to every compression design or workload.
