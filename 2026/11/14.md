#### 1. Malaysia Probes Whether AI Chips Imported by Megaspeed's Local Unit Were Re-Exported
- **Source:** [Le Temps](https://www.letemps.ch/en/world/congress-to-question-former-nvidia-boss-soon-as-malaysia-opens-investigation-into-megaspeed)
- **Published:** 2026-11-13
- **Why it matters:** The probe tests Malaysia's July 2025 AI chip export controls and puts its fast-growing data centre sector under US scrutiny.

Malaysia's Investment, Trade and Industry Ministry said it is working with the Customs Department to determine whether AI chips imported by Speedmatrix Sdn Bhd, the Malaysian subsidiary of Singapore-based data centre firm Megaspeed, were subsequently re-exported and whether any re-exports complied with the law. Speedmatrix rents data centres in Malaysia and may import AI chips without a permit, but since July 2025, exporting or transiting high-performance US AI chips from or through Malaysia requires a permit under the Strategic Trade Act 2010. The ministry stressed it has never granted approval to the subsidiary, and said Anwar Ibrahim stated there is no indication of Megaspeed-linked chips being shipped from Malaysia. The investigation follows US allegations that Megaspeed may have been used to circumvent US controls on advanced semiconductors; whether any chips were actually diverted has not been established.

#### 2. Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand and Philippines Sign Pact to Boost Local Currency Trade
- **Source:** [The Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/825673)
- **Published:** 2026-11-13
- **Why it matters:** Settling regional trade in local currencies could cut Malaysian firms' US dollar conversion costs and foreign exchange risk.

Bank Negara Malaysia, Bank Indonesia, the Bank of Thailand and Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas signed a memorandum of understanding on 13 November under the Local Currency Transaction Framework, allowing bilateral trade, investment and other eligible transactions to be settled in the four countries' own currencies. The agreement supersedes the bilateral MoUs signed by Malaysia, Indonesia and Thailand in August 2023 and brings the Philippines into the framework. The central banks said they would keep developing the framework to gradually expand local currency transactions. No timetable was given for when companies would see wider day-to-day use of the expanded arrangement.

#### 4. OpenAI Unveils Daybreak Red Team and Defense Factory for AI-Powered Cybersecurity
- **Source:** [GB Hackers](https://gbhackers.com/openai-launches-advanced-ai-cybersecurity-tools/)
- **Published:** 2026-11-13
- **Why it matters:** Pairing automated penetration testing with automated patch drafting could reshape how software vulnerabilities are found and fixed — and how much access organisations give AI agents to their code.

At its "Cyber at the Frontier" event on 12 November, OpenAI introduced Daybreak Red Team, which uses coordinated agents with source code and application context to investigate attack paths and validate vulnerabilities, and Daybreak Defense Factory, which traces findings through code and prepares patches. Defense Factory can ingest findings from existing security tools including Wiz, Snyk and Endor Labs as well as from Red Team, and engineers must review proposed changes before anything is merged — the platform does not deploy fixes automatically. Red Team is accepting early-access requests, though registration does not guarantee admission. OpenAI has not published independent performance results for either product.
