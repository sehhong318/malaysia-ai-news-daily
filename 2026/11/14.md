- **Source:** [Le Temps](https://www.letemps.ch/en/world/congress-to-question-former-nvidia-boss-soon-as-malaysia-opens-investigation-into-megaspeed)
- **Published:** 2026-11-13
- **Why it matters:** The probe tests Malaysia's July 2025 AI chip export controls and puts its fast-growing data centre sector under US scrutiny.

Malaysia's Investment, Trade and Industry Ministry said it is working with the Customs Department to determine whether AI chips imported by Speedmatrix Sdn Bhd, the Malaysian subsidiary of Singapore-based data centre firm Megaspeed, were subsequently re-exported and whether any re-exports complied with the law. Speedmatrix rents data centres in Malaysia and may import AI chips without a permit, but since July 2025, exporting or transiting high-performance US AI chips from or through Malaysia requires a permit under the Strategic Trade Act 2010. The ministry stressed it has never granted approval to the subsidiary, and said Anwar Ibrahim stated there is no indication of Megaspeed-linked chips being shipped from Malaysia. The investigation follows US allegations that Megaspeed may have been used to circumvent US controls on advanced semiconductors; whether any chips were actually diverted has not been established.

- **Published:** 2026-11-13
