#### 5. Hugging Face Switches Inference Routing to OpenRouter

- **Published:** 2026-11-16
- **Why it matters:** The two biggest open-model hubs are consolidating into one routing layer, a sign of how central open-weight models have become to AI infrastructure.

Chat models on Hugging Face Inference Providers now run through OpenRouter, which picks the provider for each request based on live price, speed and reliability, with automatic failover and retries if a provider fails. Existing Hugging Face tokens and code keep working, and 160 models from 38 labs are available this way, including the open-weight models developers use most on OpenRouter, where open-weight models carried about 70% of paid tokens in October. One breaking change: custom provider API keys no longer work for language-model requests, which are now billed to the user's Hugging Face account or organisation.

#### 6. Anthropic Cuts Vulnerability Disclosure Window to 30 Days

- **Source:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/coordinated-vulnerability-disclosure-timelines)
- **Published:** 2026-11-16
- **Why it matters:** AI is finding bugs faster than vendors patch them, and the two biggest bug-finding operations are now pressing the whole industry to fix flaws within a month.

Anthropic's Frontier Red Team is shortening its coordinated vulnerability disclosure window from 90 days to 30 days for high- and critical-severity issues, and to 45 days for all other issues, for vulnerabilities reported to vendors and maintainers on or after 1 January 2027. The change follows Google Project Zero's move to a 30-day clock on 13 November and responds to AI-accelerated bug discovery; Anthropic cited an unreleased Claude model building five exploit chains across Chrome, Safari, Android, Windows and Linux. The seven-day disclosure window for vulnerabilities under active exploitation is unchanged, and reports already in progress keep their existing deadlines.
