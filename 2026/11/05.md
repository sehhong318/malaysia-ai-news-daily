#### 1. Cyber Crime Bill gives Malaysia cross-border jurisdiction over offences targeting local systems

- **Source:** [The Vibes](https://www.thevibes.com/index.php/articles/news/129194/malaysia-can-prosecute-foreign-cybercriminals-targeting-local-systems-victims)
- **Published:** 2026-11-05
- **Why it matters:** Cross-border reach and faster evidence-preservation powers could strengthen responses to AI-enabled fraud, while making judicial safeguards and international cooperation central to enforcement.

Deputy Prime Minister Ahmad Zahid Hamidi said in a written parliamentary reply that Clause 2 of the Cyber Crime Bill 2026 allows action against offenders abroad when Malaysian computer systems or victims are involved, subject to international investigation, prosecution and extradition mechanisms. He said the bill, passed by Parliament in July to replace the Computer Crimes Act 1997, also covers digital impersonation and gives investigators powers over data preservation, disclosure, traffic data and interception; these are statutory powers subject to stated safeguards, not evidence that every overseas offender can be located or extradited.

#### 1. AutoDNA laboratory autonomously coordinates more than 11,000 biomolecular steps

- **Source:** [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11228-6)
- **Published:** 2026-11-04
- **Why it matters:** Coordinating many instruments and multi-objective workflows moves laboratory AI beyond narrow task automation toward end-to-end experimental operation.

Researchers presented AutoDNA, an AI-native biomolecular engineering laboratory that orchestrates more than 20 instrument types across over 11,000 steps for synthesis, transcription, amplification and sequencing, with people handling hardware maintenance and replenishment. Across four demonstrations, the authors report expert-comparable enzymatic DNA-synthesis yields, 3.2-times faster concurrent protocol execution, exact recovery in a DNA data-storage cycle and validation of a KRAS G13D mutation; these peer-reviewed experiments do not establish autonomous reliability across every laboratory or biological task.
