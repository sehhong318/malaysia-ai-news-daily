- **Why it matters:** Model distillation is how frontier capabilities leak to rivals — OpenAI's new defences tie a model's hidden reasoning to its conversation, making it far harder to copy.

OpenAI has detailed how it is combatting adversarial distillation — using a frontier model's outputs without authorisation to train a rival model — with strengthened protections in GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol. The company ties the models' protected reasoning to the conversation history that produced it: if an earlier message is rewritten, the API drops the reasoning that followed, and older model families cannot read the new models' reasoning. OpenAI said it has seen multiple unauthorised distillation campaigns this year, with weekly enforcement actions ranging from thousands to tens of thousands of accounts.
