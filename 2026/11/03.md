#### 2. OpenAI launches a demand-response programme for future compute campuses

- **Source:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/grid-protectors-program/)
- **Published:** 2026-11-02
- **Why it matters:** Flexible data-centre demand could reduce pressure during grid peaks, but its public benefit depends on enforceable local agreements, actual curtailment and who bears infrastructure costs.

#### 3. MIT team uses a frontier model to edit Qwen weights directly

- **Source:** [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/like-gene-editing-for-neural-networks-mit-researchers-used-gpt-6-astra-to-edit-qwens-open-weights-directly-boosting-math-performance)
- **Published:** 2026-11-02
- **Why it matters:** Reliable direct weight editing could offer a targeted alternative to conventional fine-tuning, while also increasing the need to understand and govern AI-assisted model improvement.

MIT CSAIL researchers described “weight-space programming,” in which GPT-6 Astra inspected and modified another neural network without conventional gradient-based training for the edit. In their strongest language-model example, changing about 0.016% of Qwen3.5-0.8B's weights raised its MATH-500 score from 7.9% to 48.1%, largely by correcting repetitive reasoning; the early experiment does not show that the method is broadly reliable, cheaper or better than standard training.
