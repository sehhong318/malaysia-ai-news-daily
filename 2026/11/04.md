### Malaysia News

#### 1. Malaysia declines India's request to extradite Zakir Naik

- **Source:** [The Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/824320)
- **Published:** 2026-11-03
- **Why it matters:** The parliamentary disclosure settles the government's present position on a long-running bilateral legal issue, while leaving Malaysia responsible for explaining how treaty grounds were applied.

The Home Ministry told the Dewan Rakyat that Malaysia has decided not to extradite preacher Zakir Naik and made no commitment to do so, citing its rights under Article 6 of the 2010 Malaysia-India extradition treaty and the Extradition Act 1992. It said India submitted an official request on January 19, 2018 and a diplomatic note seeking reconsideration on May 29, 2020, but Malaysia has received no new request; the decision is Malaysia's stated legal position and does not adjudicate the cases Indian authorities allege against Naik.

He also reported 1,277 defamatory items and 55,895 false-content items removed, while the Safe Internet Campaign reached more than 2.8 million participants by September 30; these are ministry-reported enforcement and outreach totals, not an independent assessment of accuracy, proportionality or impact.

- **Source:** [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/11/04/malaysias-us15b-global-sukuk-over-subscribed-47-times-parliament-told)
- **Published:** 2026-11-04
- **Why it matters:** Strong demand lowered the government's borrowing spread, but the issuance still adds debt that must be managed within statutory and fiscal limits.

Finance Minister Anwar Ibrahim said in a written parliamentary reply that the July 2026 two-tranche Global Sukuk was 4.7 times oversubscribed, enabling the government to tighten both tranches' spreads by 30 basis points from initial guidance.
