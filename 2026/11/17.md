#### 1. Google Finds AI Agents Turning Patches into Exploits with No Human in the Loop

- **Source:** [Google Cloud Blog](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/agentic-threat-activity-crosses-a-significant-threshold)
- **Published:** 2026-11-16
- **Why it matters:** It is the first observed case of attackers fully automating vulnerability discovery and exploitation, compressing the window between a patch and an attack.

Google's Threat Intelligence Group (GTIG) and Mandiant said in a report published on 16 November that they have observed threat actors using large language model frameworks to fully automate vulnerability discovery and exploitation without a human in the loop, for the first time. The researchers found a multi-agent framework they call "exploit_agent" on an exposed staging server; it compares software code before and after a patch is published, works out what the fix reveals, drafts an exploit and runs it. Victim telemetry pointed at French media, entertainment and education websites. The report also documents open-source multi-agent intrusion frameworks — ARTEX, CAIRN, CyberStrikeAI and PentAGI — in live intrusions, and says exploited zero-day flaws rose from an average of 8 a month in 2025 to 11 a month in 2026, reaching 22 in August. Google did not name the operator behind exploit_agent or say how many sites were compromised.
