#### 2. Approved Investments Rise 20.7% to RM350.4 Billion, Led by Data Centres

- **Source:** [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.com.my/2026/11/17/malaysias-investment-momentum-gets-a-rm350-billion-boost/)
- **Published:** 2026-11-17
- **Why it matters:** Data centres and cloud computing now account for more than a third of approved investment, underscoring how central the sector has become to Malaysia's growth story — and to the US chip-curb debate.

Malaysia approved RM350.4 billion of investments across the services, manufacturing and primary sectors in the first nine months of 2026, up 20.7% from RM290.3 billion a year earlier, the Malaysian Investment Development Authority (MIDA) said on 17 November. The 4,387 approved projects are expected to generate 152,090 jobs when fully implemented, with foreign investment of RM212.3 billion — 60.6% of the total — led by the United States at RM60 billion. Data centre and cloud computing projects accounted for RM123.5 billion across 86 approvals, up 44.5% year on year and 35.2% of all approved investment. Approvals are commitments rather than money already spent; MIDA said 87.4% of manufacturing projects approved between 2021 and September 2026 had reached production, factory construction or machinery installation.

#### 5. OpenAI Healthcare Agents Operate Electronic Health Record Screens Directly

- **Published:** 2026-11-17
- **Why it matters:** The agents skip EHR integration by driving the software directly, a shortcut that could cut hospital admin work — but the safety figures come from OpenAI's own evaluations, not real clinical outcomes.

OpenAI added computer-use agents, improved clinical search and more than 20 partner plugins to ChatGPT for Healthcare, the company announced on 16 November. The agents carry out multi-step tasks across healthcare software by reading screens, clicking and typing where no API exists, and organisations including Abridge, Ambience, Cigna Healthcare and CVS Health are already deploying them to process referrals and move documents between systems. Workflows involving protected health information require a Business Associate Agreement with OpenAI and specified security and data-retention settings.

#### 6. MBZUAI Releases Fully Open K2-Horizon Model Fleet

- **Source:** [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/mbzuai-opens-k2-horizon-fleet-six-models-from-09b-to-375b)
- **Published:** 2026-11-17
- **Why it matters:** The release publishes training data, checkpoints and pipelines, not just weights — the most open large-model release yet, and a direct challenge to closed labs.

The Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) released K2-Horizon on 17 November, a fleet of six open language models ranging from a 0.9-billion-parameter dense model to a 375-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts flagship. The release includes intermediate training checkpoints, roughly 10 trillion synthetic pre-training tokens and the data pipelines that produced them, following the LLM360 open-science standard — though the two largest models currently ship weights only, with full training data and code promised. The 375B model scored 65.3 on Toolathlon-Verified, which the project says leads rival open models including GLM-5.2 and MiniMax M3 and surpasses GPT-5.6 Terra on that benchmark.
