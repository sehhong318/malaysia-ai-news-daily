#### 1. Home Ministry warns Malaysia could become a transit hub for international drug syndicates

- **Source:** [New Straits Times](https://www.nst.com.my/news/nation/2026/11/1571889/malaysia-risk-becoming-transit-hub-international-drug-syndicates)
- **Published:** 2026-11-06
- **Why it matters:** The parliamentary disclosure links Malaysia's transport connectivity to a cross-border security risk and sets out a phased plan for tighter screening at KLIA and other entry points.

Home Minister Saifuddin Nasution Ismail said in a written Dewan Rakyat reply that police intelligence and operations indicate organised syndicates could use Malaysia to move drugs to local or overseas markets. He said a Cabinet-approved joint Home and Transport Ministry plan contains 17 recommendations across aviation, border and physical security, to be implemented over short-, medium- and long-term phases; the warning describes an identified risk and enforcement response, not proof that Malaysia is the hub for every regional trafficking network.

#### 2. MRT Corp awards RM2.69 billion contract for Penang LRT cross-sea extension

- **Source:** [The Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/824753)
- **Published:** 2026-11-05
- **Why it matters:** The package advances a direct island-mainland rail connection and creates an interchange with KTM, ferry and bus services at Penang Sentral.

MRT Corp awarded the Civil Main Contract 2 package to a joint venture of China Communications Construction Company, TRC Construction and Ufuk Semarak. The contract covers a 5.5km extension from Macallum to Penang Sentral, including a 3.05km sea bridge; MRT Corp said the wider Mutiara Line was 16% complete and estimated an eight-minute Komtar–Penang Sentral journey, which remains a project forecast rather than measured service performance.

#### 3. MCMC heightens monitoring after national cyber threat level rises to medium

- **Source:** [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/11/06/mcmc-on-higher-alert)
- **Published:** 2026-11-06
- **Why it matters:** Coordinated readiness across telecommunications, broadcasting, postal and courier operators is important to maintaining essential communications during heightened cyber risk.

The Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission said it had intensified monitoring and coordination with the National Security Council, National Cyber Security Agency and sector operators after the national cyber threat level was raised to medium. It urged companies to reassess vulnerabilities, detection, incident response, recovery and business continuity; the heightened level is a preparedness signal, not confirmation that a specific successful attack on Malaysian infrastructure is under way.

- **Source:** [Malay Mail/Bernama](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/11/05/pm-anwar-no-reason-to-keep-csa-secret-cabinet-to-discuss-disclosure-tomorrow/242105)

- **Why it matters:** Publication would let Parliament and the public examine the commitments supporting the government instead of relying on competing party characterisations.

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim said he had no objection to disclosing the confidence-and-supply agreement signed on October 29 with six Bersatu MPs and seven other lawmakers, and would raise the matter with Deputy Prime Minister Fadillah Yusof at the Cabinet meeting. His statement is an intention to seek further action, not confirmation that Cabinet has approved publication or that the agreement is already public.

#### 5. Malaysia postpones the 1% sustainable aviation fuel mandate to 2030

- **Source:** [The Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/824698)
- **Published:** 2026-11-05
- **Why it matters:** Deferring the mandate gives aviation operators and regulators more preparation time but delays a compulsory demand signal for lower-carbon aviation fuel.

The Plantation and Commodities Ministry said international flights departing KLIA will not be required to use a 1% sustainable aviation fuel blend from January 2027 as previously planned; the requirement is now scheduled for 2030. The ministry said the delay allows industry preparation and gives the Civil Aviation Authority of Malaysia time to build a regulatory framework, while noting existing and planned domestic production capacity; implementation in 2030 remains a future policy commitment.

### AI News

#### 1. OpenAI launches GPT-6 Cyber for approved vulnerability researchers

- **Source:** [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/05/openai-launches-gpt-6-cyber-a-gpt-6-astra-model-built-to-keep-vulnerability-research-in-scope/)
- **Published:** 2026-11-05
- **Why it matters:** A more capable cyber model can accelerate defensive discovery, while restricted access and scope controls acknowledge the corresponding offensive risk.

OpenAI released GPT-6 Cyber, a GPT-6 Astra variant for authorised vulnerability research, initially to approved Daybreak Red customers through its API and Codex. OpenAI reported 81 previously unknown high-severity Firefox vulnerabilities and a 0.07% out-of-scope rate without deployment instructions versus 5.51% for GPT-5.6-Cyber, falling to zero with instructions; these are provider-run results, and an external lab found its overall advantage over Astra narrower outside long multi-step tasks.

#### 2. Independent review estimates Anthropic's model-development pace has nearly doubled

- **Source:** [Yahoo Tech/AP](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/claude/articles/anthropic-rapidly-accelerating-ai-development-180018169.html)
- **Published:** 2026-11-05
- **Why it matters:** Evidence that AI is accelerating frontier-model research shortens the time available for safety work and makes independent access to laboratory data more consequential.

Model Evaluation and Threat Research, after reviewing Anthropic internal data and interviewing staff, estimated that capability on well-specified tasks was improving about twice as quickly as a year earlier and said AI assistance was the likely main driver. METR cautioned that the result may not generalise beyond those tasks, while Anthropic said redactions protected commercially sensitive details; the assessment did not find that AI already makes fully autonomous contributions to the most important model-training stages.

#### 4. A&O Shearman sets out a five-year AI programme valued at US$1.1 billion

- **Source:** [Non-Billable](https://www.nonbillable.co.uk/news/ao-shearman-ramps-up-ai-push-five-year-plan)
- **Published:** 2026-11-05
- **Why it matters:** Allocating lawyers' time, rather than only buying software, shows how professional-services firms are treating AI development as a core operating investment.

A&O Shearman said its five-year programme is worth US$1.1 billion, with most of the value coming from each practice devoting at least 3% of its time to specialist AI tools and services. - **Published:** 2026-11-05
- **Why it matters:** The deal consolidates real-time agent guidance, conversation analytics and autonomous customer-service agents inside a major cloud contact-centre platform.

#### 6. US Justice Department creates a Super Intelligence Task Force

- **Source:** [US Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/department-justice-launches-super-intelligence-task-force)
- **Published:** 2026-11-04
- **Why it matters:** A dedicated enforcement task force signals that advanced-AI misuse is becoming an institutional criminal-justice and national-security priority in the United States.

The Justice Department formed a task force led by Associate Attorney General Stanley Woodward and Associate Deputy Attorney General Stephen Weiss to address unlawful advanced-AI use by states, criminal organisations, individuals and companies. The department said the group would support prosecutions, regular risk reporting and stronger corporate controls; the announcement establishes an organisational mandate but gives no case list, budget or measurable enforcement timetable.
