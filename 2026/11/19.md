#### 6. Data Centres Told to Comply with US AI Chip Rules as New Curbs Take Effect

- **Source:** [Channel News Asia](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/asia/malaysia-says-data-centres-must-comply-us-ai-chip-rules-6562711)
- **Published:** 2026-11-18
- **Why it matters:** Malaysia's data centre boom — nearly US$100 billion of investment since 2021 — now has to operate under Washington's new export controls, with licensing and due-diligence requirements still unclear.

Investment, Trade and Industry Minister Johari Abdul Ghani said on 18 November that Malaysia's data centre operators should comply with new US restrictions on advanced AI chips, which he described as strategic assets. Malaysia is seeking clarification from US authorities on the licensing requirements and on rules covering the provision of data centre services to third parties, and will inform all 117 approved data centre projects once Washington officially communicates the requirements. Johari said Malaysia has yet to receive evidence that data centre tenants traded or re-exported chips in violation of US rules, and investigations into the local unit of Singapore-based cloud firm Megaspeed are ongoing.

#### 3. OpenAI Says Its Models Improved AMD's Flagship GPU Design by Up to 14%

- **Source:** [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/openai-reports-model-driven-gains-in-amd-flagship-chip-designs/)
- **Published:** 2026-11-18
- **Why it matters:** Frontier models are now measurably improving the chips they run on, compressing roughly a year of design optimisation into under five months — if OpenAI's self-reported figures hold.

OpenAI reported on 18 November that its models achieved up to a 14% improvement in power, performance and area (PPA) relative to heavily optimised baselines on AMD's flagship GPU intellectual property, by rewriting the SystemVerilog code that describes the chip's circuits. Some of the changes have been included in a design AMD has sent for manufacturing, while others were added to production design files for future products, and AMD also verified single-digit percentage PPA gains on its next-generation flagship CPU. OpenAI also said model-proposed changes cut standard-cell area by about 13% in the vector unit and 10% in the matrix unit of Jalapeño, its first custom inference chip, and that it is partnering with Synopsys to train a model, GPT-Synopsys, to use electronic design automation tools. The figures are self-reported by OpenAI and AMD and have not been independently verified.
